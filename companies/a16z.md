## What It Is

**Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)** is one of the largest and most influential US venture-capital firms (founded 2009 by **Marc Andreessen** and **Ben Horowitz**), and a central capital + narrative force in the AI buildout. The wiki tracks it as an **AI-infrastructure and applied-AI investor**, as a **policy actor** (co-lead of the open-weights coalition), and as a **narrative platform** (the a16z podcast). This page was created (2026-08-21) to anchor the DOJ antitrust investigation and to resolve a16z's long-standing recurring-reference presence across the wiki.

## Why the Wiki Tracks It

- **Policy actor — open-weights coalition co-lead**: a16z co-leads (with **NVIDIA** and **Palantir**) the *"Open Weights and American AI Leadership"* coalition letter ([[open-weights-american-ai-leadership-coalition-2026-07-24]], grown to 230+ signatories) — the pro-open, anti-over-regulation pole of the [[frontier-ai-governance|governance landscape]]. Core position: *"keep the frontier plural."*
- **Narrative platform**: the **a16z podcast** hosts the founder-thesis conversations the wiki tracks — e.g. [[garry-tan-new-rules-for-founders-a16z-2026-08-13|Garry Tan's "New Rules for Founders"]] (Anish Acharya × Tan) that anchors the [[engineering-leadership-ai-era|experienced-founder wave]] thread.
- **AI-infra + applied-AI investor**: recurring board-seat and capital presence across the AI-infrastructure stack — the concentration the DOJ probe (below) targets.

## Traction Signals

- **2026-08-21 — DOJ antitrust investigation into board conflicts** ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-08-21]], TechCrunch): the **DOJ is investigating a16z under a 112-year-old antitrust statute** (Clayton Act §8, **interlocking directorates** — the same person/firm sitting on the boards of competitors). Reported as the **first VC-focused action under the long-dormant statute**; the signal is **regulatory pressure on concentrated AI-infrastructure board seats** (a VC holding board positions across competing AI-infra companies). Outcome unclear, but a novel front: antitrust scrutiny aimed at the *investor/board* layer rather than the labs. Pairs with the [[frontier-ai-governance|governance]] thread's **incumbent-concentration / regulatory-capture** watch. *(Investigation, not a charge; TechCrunch podcast source.)*
