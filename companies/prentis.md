## What It Is

**Prentis** is a new AI lab **co-founded by Reid Hoffman and Mark Pincus**, reportedly **in talks to raise $100M** (TechCrunch, 2026-07-24). Its wedge: **automating routine computer tasks** — the bet that *"automation, not coding, is the killer AI wedge"* because it doesn't require the user to *"think like an engineer."* *(Single-source; primary not fetched.)*

- **2026-07-24 — ~$100M raise in talks** ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-07-25]], TechCrunch): notable-founder signal (**Reid Hoffman** + **Mark Pincus**) on the automation-first thesis. The [[dailybrief-roundup-2026-07-25|brief]]'s read: *"betting on scale, not SKU innovation."*
