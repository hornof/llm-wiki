## What It Is

**DiscoLoop AI** (@DiscoLoopAI) is a new AI startup announced **2026-08-06**, co-founded by **[[jeff-dean|Jeff Dean]]** on his departure from Google (after 27 years) alongside longtime collaborators **Sanjay Ghemawat**, **Oriol Vinyals**, and **Quoc Le**. The founding team is one of the most decorated in the field — between them: **MapReduce / Bigtable / Spanner** (Dean + Ghemawat), **TensorFlow / Google Brain**, **AlphaStar / AlphaFold / Gemini co-lead** (Vinyals), and **seq2seq / AutoML / the original Google Brain scaling work** (Le). Details of the company's focus were not disclosed in the announcement.
