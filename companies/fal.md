## What It Is

fal runs inference for generative media models (image, video, audio and 3D) for developers, studios, enterprises and the labs that build the models. Founded in 2021 by Burkay Gür (CEO) and Görkem Yurtseven (CTO), who previously built ML infrastructure at Coinbase and Amazon. It began as Python data infrastructure and pivoted to image-model inference after Stable Diffusion. About 150 employees ([[fal-1-65b-series-e-f-2026-11-03]]).

## Traction Signals

- **2026-11-03: $1.65B combined Series E and F** (Sequoia led E, Bessemer led F; valuation not stated). Prior round: $140M Series D at $4.5B in December 2025 ([[fal-1-65b-series-e-f-2026-11-03]]; first seen as a headline in [[dailybrief-roundup-2026-11-05]]).
- **Revenue mix (founder claim):** video went from zero to about 70% of revenue, and is now 50/50 with image after image-editing models arrived.
- **Serves the labs as well as apps.** Gür says labs now line up inference capacity before a launch, and many partner with fal for it. fal hosted OpenAI's Sora before Sora was taken down.

## How It Runs (founder claims, 2026-11-03)

Relevant to the owner's org-design interest. At most two reporting levels and no pure managers; leads are "super ICs" who build and lead. Yurtseven estimates coding agents make engineering 3 to 4× faster, so fal would need about 4× the headcount without them. Gür expects each technical leader to supervise 5 to 10 engineers while coding agents keep improving. Crunch periods run 60 to 80 hours a week. All from one interview; no independent check.

## Compared To

- **LLM inference.** fal's founders say they avoided the open LLM market because token prices were falling below cost, and focused on image and video instead.
