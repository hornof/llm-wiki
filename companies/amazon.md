## What It Is

**Amazon** is a US technology + cloud + retail company; the wiki tracks it primarily as an **AI-infrastructure and frontier-lab-investment** player through **AWS** (Amazon Web Services), not for its consumer/retail business. Led by CEO **[[andy-jassy|Andy Jassy]]**.
