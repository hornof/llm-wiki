## What It Is

Mercor sells the data, evaluations and reinforcement-learning environments that labs use to train models on professional work, produced by expert contractors it recruits. It was founded in 2023 and began as AI-powered recruiting ([[mercor-750m-series-d-20b-2026-11]]). It sits in the same market as Handshake's AI-training business and the other expert-data vendors in [[data-as-moat-frontier-2026-05-30]].
