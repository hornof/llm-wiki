## What It Is

**Claude Science** is an [[anthropic|Anthropic]] AI workbench for scientists — a customizable app that integrates research tools, produces auditable artifacts, and provides compute access, aimed at running research workflows on top of Claude. Announced June 30, 2026 and available as of the [[dailybrief-roundup-2026-07-06|2026-07-06 brief]] "From the Labs" item.

## Traction Signals
- **2026-10-27: Divergence Labs' IgE preprint, the primary behind one of those case studies** ([[divergence-labs-ige-claude-antibody-2026-10-27]]). Of 91 disruptor designs tested, 20 were active, and three removed more IgE than omalizumab in a dose-titration assay. Claude broke the objective down and chose the redesign phases, with humans guiding. Lab assays only. The paper doesn't say the work ran in Claude Science.

- Vendor-announced product launch; no independent adoption signals captured (2026-07-06). Status held at `emerging`.

The named tools are Claude Code and Claude connected to partners' own systems; Claude Science is mentioned as the workbench Anthropic has *"rolled out"* alongside credits for life scientists. So the post is evidence for Claude in drug development, not yet for this product.
