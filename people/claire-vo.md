## Who They Are

Claire Vo is a product-and-engineering leader turned AI-native builder. She created **ChatPRD** (an AI product-management tool that became the wiki's canonical *"$1M, one-employee"* solo-founder receipt — see [[ai-native-organizations]]), hosts the **"How I AI"** podcast (100+ episodes interviewing practitioners on real AI workflows), and was the *"AI-pilled exec"* at LaunchDarkly (prior CPO/CTO roles). Worth tracking as a **hands-on AI-transformation practitioner** whose own career arc is a live datapoint on how the solo-founder / AI-native-org theses actually play out.

## Their Current Focus

- **Launching CXO.dev — AI-transformation consulting "by builders, not consultants" (2026-08-25)** ([[clairevo-cxo-dev-launch-2026-08-25]]): Vo **gives up the solo-founder hat** to co-found CXO.dev with **Zach Davis** (2 decades IC + eng leadership at Atlassian and LaunchDarkly). The pitch: *"AI operating models for scaled software companies"* — helping *"AI-pilled leaders"* actually change how their teams ship. Her mental model for what real team-level change requires is three-part: **technical readiness + an agent-ready operating model + (the hard one) cultural change** — *"there was so much more than giving people Claude or ChatGPT and calling it a day."* Three service lines: (1) build the AI-transformation roadmap, (2) work with eng/product/design to change how they ship (incl. **getting the repo agent-ready**), (3) coach/train teams on tools they already have. Early customers: **Amplitude, Chime, GCAI.** *(Launch-day promotional thread; capability claims self-asserted.)*

## Notable Takes

- **"Giving people Claude isn't transformation"** — the load-bearing insight behind CXO.dev: tool access ≠ adoption; the work is in the code (repo readiness), the operating model, and culture. The builder-led counter-positioning to traditional consulting (*"by builders, not consultants"*).
- **The solo-founder poster child pivots to a team** — Vo was the [[ai-native-organizations|ChatPRD one-person-$1M]] exemplar; her move *back* to a co-founded, network-of-operators consulting firm is a narrative reversal worth tracking against the "solo founder is the future" thesis. Pairs with [[alex-lieberman|Alex Lieberman's]] @businessbarista AI-transformation practice as a **second builder-led AI-transformation-consulting entrant** in the same window.
- **"The skeptic and the chaos agent"** — her framing of pairing an AI-pilled exec with a veteran skeptical eng leader (Zach Davis) to drive adoption; both perspectives load-bearing (cf. [[simon-willison|Charity Majors' enthusiasts-vs-skeptics]] framing).
