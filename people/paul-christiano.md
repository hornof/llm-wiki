## Who They Are

Head of the Alignment Research Center. Previously head of AI safety at the US AI Safety Institute, and before that led alignment at OpenAI, where Dwarkesh Patel's introduction credits him with inventing RLHF. Had recently joined OpenAI's board as of 2026-11-16 ([[christiano-dwarkesh-takeover-12-months-2026-11-16]]). Worth tracking because he is a senior alignment researcher who now sits on a frontier lab's board and puts numbers on near-term risk.

## Their Current Focus

Near-term takeover risk and lab security. On Dwarkesh's podcast (2026-11-16) he argued that labs should pause algorithmic progress until they can protect their weights and methods from theft ([[christiano-dwarkesh-takeover-12-months-2026-11-16]]).

## Notable Takes

- **Takeover risk within a year is "more like a 10% risk than a 1% risk"**, higher if developers race without precautions; about a one-in-three chance of superintelligence within a year if nothing slows (2026-11-16, [[christiano-dwarkesh-takeover-12-months-2026-11-16]]).
- **Pause algorithmic progress until security is adequate.** His argument is national security: improvements that leak help an adversary who would use them more carelessly (same source).
- **No current alignment approach has better than even odds** of controlling superintelligence; the hope is that AIs solve it during the intelligence explosion (same source).
