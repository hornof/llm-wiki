## Who They Are

Machine-learning researcher who writes the long-running blog *Machine Learning (Theory)* at hunch.net. He was a founding leader of AI Frontiers (AIF), a roughly 50-person research sub-organization inside Microsoft Research formed in 2023 after ChatGPT's release ([[langford-end-of-ai-frontiers-2026-11-06]]). Worth tracking as a research-side witness to how frontier-lab orgs are choosing between scaling and research.

## Their Current Focus

- **2026-11-06: announced the end of AI Frontiers.** AIF was reorganized into Microsoft AI in early September 2026. Langford left, saying the rest of AIF's founding leadership did too. His next role isn't stated.
- AIF work he names: the Autogen orchestration framework, the Fara computer-use agent, the Next Latent objective, Dion3 (an improved Muon optimizer) and the Full Bandwidth Transformer.

## Notable Takes

- **Scaling-only is a mistake, and he puts a number on what research is worth.** He calls MAI's ex-Inflection leadership philosophy *"scaling, and aggressively not research"*. He cites ByteDance experiments suggesting a Full Bandwidth Transformer variant *"might be comparable to a model of twice the size"*, and asks what halving training and inference cost is worth ([[langford-end-of-ai-frontiers-2026-11-06]]).
- **Research with no path to use isn't worth doing.** That is his stated reason for not returning to MSR ([[langford-end-of-ai-frontiers-2026-11-06]]).
