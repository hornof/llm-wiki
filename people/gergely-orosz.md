## Who They Are

Gergely Orosz (@GergelyOrosz) writes **The Pragmatic Engineer**, the most-read newsletter on software engineering and engineering management (ex-Uber, ex-Skype). His beat is the *practice and economics of building software teams* — hiring, compensation, org design, and how engineering roles are changing. A high-signal, field-sourced voice (he reports from direct conversations with practitioners), which makes him a useful counterweight to vendor and hype narratives on what AI is actually doing to engineering work and careers.

## Their Current Focus

- **2026-07-27 — CTO/VPE burnout & exodus in the AI era** ([[gergely-orosz-eng-leadership-exodus-2026-07-27]]): reports that engineering-leadership roles at startups/mid-sized companies are churning — unrealistic AI-era expectations, fear of missing AI-native experience, and better fractional/founder alternatives are driving leaders out. Anchor for [[engineering-leadership-ai-era]]. Framed carefully as a small-n field observation (3–4 people, mostly SF/NYC), not a definitive claim.

## Notable Takes

- **The eng-leadership role is structurally squeezed right now**: higher expectations, unclear AI strategy at many companies, and a real risk of *"career suicide"* staying somewhere that can't give you AI-native experience — so strong leaders increasingly go fractional or found.
- Consistently **field-sourced and hedged**: names sample size and geography, resists over-generalizing — the register that makes his read trustworthy.
