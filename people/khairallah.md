## Their Current Focus

- **2026-06-23: "How to become an AI engineer in 2026" career guide** ([[khairallah-how-to-become-ai-engineer-without-cs-degree-2026-06-23]]). The thesis is that a portfolio beats credentials, with a six-phase roadmap. It separates two roles: *"You're aiming to become the engineer who builds with AI, not the scientist who builds the AI"*. The AI engineer sits where software engineering, a working grasp of how LLMs behave, and product thinking meet. Python and Git are required from day one, and math can wait.
- **2026-06-06: Claude Projects six-part blueprint** ([[khairallah-claude-projects-6-part-blueprint-2026-06-06]]). Identity, rules, process, output format, knowledge files and onboarding blocks, plus three failure modes. Claimed payoff: a 45-minute setup saves about six work-weeks a year (unverified).
- **2026-05-31: "a system that prompts itself" video pitch** ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-05-31]]). The hooks are unverified: *"the 14% you lose to CLAUDE.md"*, *"28 untouched features"*, and one prompt then closing the tab is *"leaving 90% on the table"*. The useful idea is the self-prompting system. Its push to adopt every feature is the opposite of [[nate-herk|Nate Herk's]] view that most knowledge work doesn't need a workflow.
- **2026-05-15: multi-agent team course** ([[eng-khairallah-multi-agent-team-course-2026-05-15]]). Pipeline, fan-out and specialist-team patterns, a 20-agent ceiling, and Netflix, Harvey and Shopify cited as production users (unverified).

## Notable Takes

- **Portfolio over degree** (06-23): *"a portfolio of shipped projects carries more weight than a degree"*.
- **Researcher versus engineer** (06-23): ML researchers invent and train new models, which takes advanced degrees and heavy math. AI engineers build useful things with models that already exist, which takes software skill, product sense and shipping.
- **Defer the math** (06-23): *"You do not need to master linear algebra and calculus to start building with LLMs."*
- **Roadmap** (06-23): Phase 1 (months 1–3) is learning to code properly, and Phase 2 (months 3–5) is mastering the LLM API. Phases 3–6 weren't captured.
