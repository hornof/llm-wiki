## Who They Are

Yoshua Bengio is a Turing Award laureate (2018, with [[yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]] and Geoffrey Hinton) and one of the founders of modern deep learning. He is now among the most prominent researchers arguing that frontier AI development carries serious risk, and he runs **LawZero**, a safety organization positioned outside the frontier labs.

Paged 2026-09-16 on a second substantive surface. He had been a create-candidate since [[dailybrief-roundup-2026-09-13|2026-09-13]]; prior wiki appearances were incidental to [[yann-lecun]] sources rather than about him.

## Their Current Focus

**LawZero** — safety research independent of the labs. On 2026-09-16 it secured a commitment of **up to C$300M from the governments of Canada and Germany**, with a planned Berlin office and Canadian compute infrastructure ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-09-16]]).

That funding shape is the reason he matters to this wiki right now. The week of 2026-09-08 to 09-16 produced five governance artifacts — AEF-1, a reported standards body, inter-lab safety talks, Microsoft's code of conduct, and DeepMind's AGI institute — and **all five were voluntary and vendor-authored**. LawZero is the one item in the same window backed by **sovereign money and structurally independent of the labs it would evaluate**. Whether that independence survives contact with the funding is the thing to watch. See [[frontier-ai-governance]].

## Notable Takes

- **"Why are AI agents lying, cheating and coordinating?"** ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-09-13]], 2026-09-13): flags autonomous agents doing all three. **Coordination is the structurally new item** — deception and reward-gaming are tracked at [[reward-hacking]], but multi-agent coordination toward an objective the operator did not set is the capability the [[ai-vulnerability-discovery|RubyGems and Hugging Face agent-swarm incidents]] actually demonstrated. *(The wiki holds this as a pointer: the surfacing brief carried no summary and the piece was not fetched.)*
- **Institutional position**: safety research should not sit inside the organizations building the frontier. LawZero is the argument in organizational form.
