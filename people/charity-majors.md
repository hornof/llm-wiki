## Who They Are

**Charity Majors** is co-founder and CTO of **Honeycomb**, an observability platform, and an author of *Observability Engineering* ([[willison-charity-majors-enthusiasts-skeptics-2026-06-04]]). The wiki tracks Majors as an engineering-management voice on AI adoption, mostly through [[simon-willison|Simon Willison]] quoting her. All the wiki's material dates from June 2026.

## Their Current Focus

- **2026-06-17: what AI does to the economics of code** ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-06-18]]). Willison quotes Majors, as the brief gives it: *"Structural shift: code went from scarce/precious to disposable/regenerable. Engineering discipline now matters differently."* Willison's post was not fetched, so the exact wording is the brief's. The same day, Rahul GS argued that the cost of complexity is changing ([[rahulgs-english-code-interpreters-10-point-thesis-2026-06-17]]).

## Notable Takes

- **"AI enthusiasts are in a race against time, AI skeptics are in a race against entropy."** (2026-06-04, via Willison; [[willison-charity-majors-enthusiasts-skeptics-2026-06-04]]). Both sides are right about different things: enthusiasts that the window for advantage closes, skeptics that systems decay without maintenance. The wiki's reading is that teams fail when they pick one side instead of running both. That fits the [[ai-roi-gap]] and the [[uber-ai-tool-cap-1500-2026-06-03|Uber spending cap]], where the budget control came after the bill.
- **Code is now cheap to produce, so discipline moves** (2026-06-17, above). If code can be regenerated, the engineering effort goes into what stays scarce: specification, testing, observability. That last step is the wiki's inference, not Majors' words.
