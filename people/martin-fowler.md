## Who They Are

Martin Fowler writes martinfowler.com, a long-running site on software architecture, refactoring and delivery, and works at Thoughtworks (the site's footer links it). Their site also hosts other authors' writing on generative AI, such as Birgitta Böckeler's series on spec-driven development ([[heeki-spec-driven-development-2026-02-28]]). *[unsourced: their books and role at Thoughtworks aren't covered by an ingested source.]*

They're worth tracking as a senior engineering voice who is skeptical of handing writing and thinking to LLMs, which bears on how the owner writes.

## Their Current Focus

- **2026-10-29 — "LLM Voice"** ([[fowler-llm-voice-2026-10-29]]): readers recoil from LLM-written prose, and editing an LLM draft rarely removes the voice. Fowler's advice is to write the prose yourself and use an LLM at most for an outline. Their site asks authors to declare any AI use.

## Notable Takes

- **Writing is thinking** (10-29): *"As more people delegate writing to LLMs they will also delegate thinking and comprehension to LLMs."*
- **Detectors miss the point** (10-29): the reaction to LLM prose is human, and a tool's verdict comes too late.
