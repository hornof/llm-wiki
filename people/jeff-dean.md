## Who They Are

Jeff Dean (@JeffDean) is one of the most influential systems-and-ML engineers in computing history — at Google from **1999 to 2026** (joining when it was ~25 people; leaving at 190,000+). His fingerprints are on the infrastructure that runs the modern internet: **MapReduce, Bigtable, Spanner, TensorFlow**, the TPU program, **Google Brain** (which he co-founded), and later **Chief Scientist of Google DeepMind** and a leader on **Gemini**. Surfaced into the wiki on **2026-08-06** when he announced his departure to co-found **[[discoloop-ai|DiscoLoop AI]]**.

## Their Current Focus

- **2026-08-06 — leaves Google after 27 years to co-found [[discoloop-ai|DiscoLoop AI]]** ([[raw-batch-roundup-2026-08-06]]): with **Sanjay Ghemawat, Oriol Vinyals, and Quoc Le.** In his farewell note: *"Google now has thirteen products used by more than a billion people."* No details yet on DiscoLoop's focus. A major departure from [[google-deepmind]] at the top of its research org.

## Notable Takes

- His body of work *is* the take: the through-line is **scale** — MapReduce/Bigtable made web-scale data tractable; Google Brain + TensorFlow + TPUs made deep-learning-at-scale tractable; the same instinct now aimed at a startup. Worth tracking for what DiscoLoop signals about where the systems-first-scaling school of thought goes next.
