## Who They Are

**Samuel McDonald** (@samueljmcd) is an independent practitioner-content voice on X, wiki-tracked as the **verifier-discipline-first corrective** in the [[loop-engineering|Loop Engineering]] discourse. His 2026-06-15 essay reframed the whole "loops" conversation around the claim that **the verifier — not the generator — is the bottleneck**. Promoted to an entity page at the 2026-09-10 lint.

## Their Current Focus

- **2026-06-15: "My Thoughts on Loop Engineering"** ([[samueljmcd-loop-engineering-verifier-bottleneck-2026-06-15]]) — a structurally major corrective to the loops discourse. Core reformulation: *"A loop is a generator wired to a verifier. The generator was never the bottleneck. The verifier is."* Closing directive: ***"Design the verifier, not the prompt."***

## Notable Takes

- **Verifier is the bottleneck** — *"loop engineering is the new label; the hard part is the one it's always been: verification."*
- **Open vs Closed loops** — open loops (goal + freedom) are novelty + slop-machine + budget-burner; **closed loops** (goal + defined steps + eval-at-each-step + stop condition) are what produce production-tier results today. *"The autonomy is not the reason. The evaluation gate is."*
- **Inner vs Outer loop** — inner (single-task verify) is mature; the **outer loop** (persistent lesson-carry-forward across sessions, `SKILL.md`/`AGENTS.md`) is *"still half-built."*
- **"A loop that goes green is not a loop that is correct"** — output quality is capped by verifier quality, "not one point higher."
- **Management framing** — *"management in the age of agents is about designing the constraints they run inside, the same as it always was with people."*
