## Who They Are

Travis Oliphant is one of the foundational figures of the scientific-Python ecosystem: **creator of NumPy** (and SciPy), and **co-founder of Anaconda** (the Python data-science distribution) and Quansight. His historical playbook — *create an open standard, drive adoption through trust and ecosystem participation, capture the commercial value of the network that forms around it* — is precisely the one he is now applying to enterprise AI infrastructure through **[[openteams|OpenTeams / Nebari]]**. Surfaced into the wiki via the June 2026 *"Distributed AI Economy"* whitepaper ([[openteams-distributed-ai-economy-whitepaper-2026-06]]).

## Their Current Focus

- **[[openteams|OpenTeams / Nebari]] — "the Distributed AI Economy"** (2026-06): building open-source AI infrastructure (Nebari + Nebi) plus a three-layer *Intelligence Hub / Frames-Cogs-Ops / marketplace* architecture for **sovereign, owned enterprise AI**. The pitch leans explicitly on his prior track record: *"NumPy standardized arrays, Anaconda distributed the Python data science stack, Nebari is standardizing AI infrastructure."* Working with Dharhas Pothina (Nebari lead).

## Notable Takes

- **Open standards compound trust**: the same dynamic that made NumPy the universal array standard (and enabled the scientific-Python ecosystem) is his stated moat for AI infrastructure — *"when the infrastructure layer is open, trust compounds and adoption accelerates."*
- **Own your intelligence**: aligns with [[reverse-information-paradox|Nadella's sovereignty argument]] — organizational context/memory *"belongs to the organization that produced it… it cannot be rented or bought from a vendor."*
