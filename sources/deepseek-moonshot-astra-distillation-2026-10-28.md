## Summary

The Information reports, and Reuters relays, that DeepSeek and Moonshot AI relied heavily on outputs from OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to train their October models: Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash 1017. The report sets this beside Anthropic's crackdown on distillation of Claude. Reuters is paywalled, so this page uses Digg's summary of the Reuters account. Reported 2026-10-28.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The claim:** DeepSeek and Moonshot *"relied heavily on outputs from OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to train their latest models"*, citing *"people with direct knowledge of the matter"*.
- **Models named:** Kimi K3.1 Pro (released the preceding week) and DeepSeek V4.1-Flash 1017 (launched 2026-10-17).
- **Anthropic's number:** its safeguards *"reduced data harvested in distillation attacks by about 90% to 99%"*, per Reuters.
- **Causation isn't established.** Digg: *"The reporting does not establish that Anthropic's measures drove either lab to Astra."* Social posts asserting that Anthropic's crackdown pushed the labs to Astra go further than the reporting.
- **No responses:** OpenAI, DeepSeek and Moonshot didn't immediately reply to Reuters.
