## Summary

[[anthropic|Anthropic]]'s launch post for Claude Haiku 5.5 (2026-10-07), its small, fast model. The wiki had seen Haiku 5.5 only in passing (a game demo and METR's 11-05 assessment) and had no launch date or price for it. This page was fetched to give the Anthropic model table a Haiku row. Benchmarks and customer quotes are Anthropic's.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Positioning:** *"the cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model we’ve ever released."* It's meant for *"high-volume, cost-sensitive tasks"* and *"pairs well with Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 as a subagent on coding work."*
- **Price:** *"$0.10 / $0.50"* per million input tokens for prompts up to / over 100k, and *"$0.50 / $2.50"* for output. Haiku 4.5 was *"$1.00"* input and *"$5.00"* output. Anthropic says *"On average, it now costs around 75% less to run."*
- **Effort setting:** *"our first Haiku-class model to come with an adjustable effort setting."*
- **Benchmarks (Anthropic's), Haiku 5.5 vs Sonnet 5.5:** Terminal-Bench 4.0 *"39.2%"* vs *"70.6%"*; OSWorld 2.1 (offline subset) *"72.4%"* vs *"83.9%"*. Haiku 4.5 scored *"0.0%"* on Terminal-Bench 4.0.
- **Same-day changes:** Anthropic is *"halving the price of Claude Sonnet 5.5’s cache reads"* and adding *"a new monthly API credit for our Claude Max, Ultra, and Team subscribers"*.
- **Availability:** *"available now on all platforms, including Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure"*; model ID `claude-haiku-5-5`.
- **For the owner:** at a tenth of Haiku 4.5's input price, it's the default choice for subagents, classification and summarization in tools like ai-pulse. [Judgment.]
