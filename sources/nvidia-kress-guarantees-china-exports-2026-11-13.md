## Summary

Two Digg summaries that followed [[nvidia|Nvidia]]'s CEO change ([[nvidia-ceo-transition-2026-11-12]]). The first (https://digg.com/tech/djs874oo) relays The Wall Street Journal and Reuters on how [[jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] treated Colette Kress, now interim CEO, and on her pushback against Nvidia's financing guarantees for chip buyers. The second (https://digg.com/tech/9vaqzvo4) relays The Information's allegation that 240,000 Nvidia chips reached China despite US export controls. Neither underlying article was read; Digg says The Information's body was paywalled. As on the 11-12 page, this page doesn't name the private individuals in the reports.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Huang on Kress (WSJ, one unnamed source):** in an August finance meeting Huang called her *"“a deer in the headlights,”"* and described her as *"a “reporting CFO,” not someone who could help him define strategy or make decisions"*.
- **The guarantees:** *"Nvidia was considering guarantees worth billions of dollars for companies that couldn’t afford its hardware."* The deals offered *"financing to emerging cloud providers and data-center developers to cultivate long-term customers."* Kress *"questioned which deals Nvidia should pursue, how to value the guarantees and how to calculate investment returns"*.
- **OpenAI's Ohio data center:** per the Journal, *"Kress reworked the arrangement and cut Nvidia’s initial financial commitment in half"*. The smaller deal *"was signed on August 17."* Reuters separately reports clashes over commitments to OpenAI and Lambda.
- **After that:** *"Nvidia also paused some revenue-sharing credit-support deals in late August."* *"Neither the Journal nor Reuters reports a new financing policy under Kress."* In public she defended the approach: *"we know some will call this circular financing. We see it differently."*
- **The export allegation (The Information):** a group of companies called Litong Inc *"has imported 240,000 Nvidia chips worth at least $7.5 billion into China since 2023 despite U.S. export controls"*, mostly *"H100 and H200 systems."* Some reportedly reached data centers serving Alibaba and Tencent through Century Litong, *"an Nvidia preferred cloud partner"*. Digg: *"The shipments and valuation have not been independently verified"*, and the partner label *"does not establish that Nvidia knew about prohibited shipments."*
- **Why it matters here:** the guarantee story is the vendor-financing loop in [[ai-margin-collapse]] seen from inside Nvidia, with the CFO as the brake. [Judgment.]
