## Summary

A Digg aggregation (2026-10-23) of Bloomberg's report, relayed by Cointelegraph and Rohan Paul: [[deepseek]] has raised about **$15B**, with Tencent and CATL among the largest backers. CATL reportedly put in ~$1.5B, double its first check. Bloomberg itself wasn't read, and there's no company confirmation.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Round history (reported):**
  - A first round of more than $7.5B closed in June 2026.
  - The target for this round rose at least twice, from ~$7.5B to $12B, before reaching ~$15B.
  - About $22.5B in total over roughly six months.
- **Valuation:** ~$74.8B pre-money per Chinese media, unchanged from the first round even though the round doubled. *(Unconfirmed.)*
- **Use of funds:** data centers and open-weight models.
- **Noise to discard:** an ns123abc "BREAKING" post on the page is satire, and its $7B/$11B figures aren't reporting.
- **Against the wiki's record:** [[deepseek]] recorded a *reported fundraise pause* on 2026-07-22, after a leaked investor meeting. If the June close is right, the pause came after a round had already closed, or the pause report was wrong. Both claims are recorded.
