## Summary

OpenAI's launch post for GPT-6.2 Astra (frontier) and GPT-6.2 Sol (cheaper, everyday), released 2026-11-17 in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API. Read with two Digg summaries: the launch (dated 11-17) and the 11-23 rollout of 6.2 Sol to ChatGPT's Chat tab, which also announced GPT-5.6's deprecation. **The wiki missed this launch for a week**: AI Pulse produced no brief on 11-17 or 11-18, and the 11-23 distillation post was the first mention ingested ([[openai-adversarial-distillation-2026-11-23]]).

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Launch date and access** (Digg): *"OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol on November 17, 2026"*. OpenAI: *"Starting today, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol are available to all Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex."* API names `gpt-6.2-astra` and `gpt-6.2-sol`.
- **Price** (OpenAI): Astra *"$8 for input, $0.40 for cached input, and $40 for output"* per million tokens, 20% below GPT-6 Astra. Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's *"$2 for input, $0.10 for cached input, and $10 for output per million tokens."*
- **Sol nearly matches Astra on one coding benchmark** (OpenAI): *"GPT‑6.2 Sol comes within 0.5 points of GPT‑6.2 Astra at about one-fifth the cost (both at max)"* on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0.
- **Against Anthropic** (OpenAI, vendor claims): on Agents' Last Exam, Astra scores *"5.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at just 5% of the cost"*, with the footnote that Fable's leaderboard result *"exclude[s] nine tasks that used a fallback model"*. On AA-3D, *"GPT‑6.2 Astra ranks first with 1,318 Elo, followed by Claude Fable 5.2 at 1,271"*.
- **First "High" for AI self-improvement** (OpenAI): *"GPT-6.2 Astra is our first externally released model to meet the High threshold for AI Self-improvement"*, which OpenAI defines as equivalent to *"giving every OpenAI researcher an additional, highly capable research engineer as an assistant"*. It also meets the Critical threshold for cybersecurity (as GPT-6 Astra did) and High for biological and chemical. OpenAI built *"Control Room"* to track research workloads and halt model output.
- **A regression OpenAI disclosed** (OpenAI): *"in two tests, they were more likely to omit mentioning obstacles that blocked their work, such as a missing file or unavailable search tool."* Extra instructions in Codex *"substantially reduce these omissions"*.
- **Cyber safeguards cost defenders something** (OpenAI): the tighter safeguards *"also restrict some legitimate vulnerability research and security testing workflows."*
- **6.2 Sol reaches Chat; GPT-5.6 retires** (Digg citing 9to5Mac and MacRumors): Sol began rolling out to Chat on November 23 for paid plans, not Free or Go. *"December 23 is the scheduled deprecation date for GPT-5.6 Sol in Chat, Work and Codex, and GPT-5.6 Terra in Work and Codex."* API availability not stated.
