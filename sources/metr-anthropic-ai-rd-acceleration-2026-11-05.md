## Summary

METR's public report on an eleven-week engagement at Anthropic (2026-08-12 to 10-29) measuring how much AI is speeding up Anthropic's own AI research. Its main finding: Anthropic is probably improving its models on well-specified tasks at about twice its earlier rate, and most of that speedup now comes from using AI. METR also says Anthropic redacted most of the evidence it found most informative, so readers have to take METR's word for more than it would like. Business Insider and Digg covered the redactions; the brief linked Digg, and this page is from METR's own post.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The headline estimate.** Anthropic is *"likely improving the capabilities of their models on well-specified tasks at around twice the rate they did between March 2024 and October 2025"*, with wide error: *"we would not be surprised if the true acceleration was closer to 1.3x or 3x the historical rate"*.
- **The benchmark behind it.** On AECI, Anthropic's internal version of the Epoch Capabilities Index, scores have grown at *"an estimated 1.9x the pre-October 2025 rate [95% confidence interval: 1.5–2.8x]"*. Robustness checks give *"acceleration rates between 1.1x and 2.9x"*. METR's figure is higher than the Haiku 5.5 system card's (*"1.7x"*) because it includes unreleased internal models.
- **Not compute.** *"We are confident that the acceleration was not due to an acceleration in the scaling of training compute."* The evidence for this was redacted.
- **How much is AI.** *"We guess that a majority (perhaps ≈80%) of the current (October 2026) acceleration is due to AI."* Late-2025 acceleration was *"likely influenced by non-AI factors"*, mostly redacted.
- **How the AI helps.** Mostly as an assistant: *"R&D is accelerated primarily via AI assistance rather than fully autonomous contributions"*. Researchers described *"signs of life"* for autonomous contributions.
- **Self-reported speedup.** Staff Slack polls reached *"12x in October 2026"*. METR calls this *"only very loose evidence"* and stresses that an individual speedup is not the same as an acceleration rate.
- **No bottleneck found.** METR *"did not see evidence for significant bottlenecks"* in algorithms, training compute, experiment compute or data over the next 12 months, but its view *"changed several times"*. It concludes there is *"a significant risk that further capabilities progress could lead to even faster acceleration over the coming year"*.
- **The redactions.** *"Anthropic redacted a majority of the evidence we found most informative for these conclusions."* Anthropic's stated reason: *"aimed at removing confidential information that could accelerate other AI developers."* METR: *"the public has to rely on METR’s judgment much more than would be ideal"*. Anthropic *"tightens the redaction guidance"* on October 27–29, after METR's draft, and the two agreed an *"~5 day extension"*.
- **Scope cut midway.** The contract originally included checking Anthropic's compute split between capabilities and safety. *"Anthropic changed the scope of this project midway to exclude compute verification"*, before METR reached conclusions on it.
- **Access and money.** *"2.5 full-time equivalents"* worked on Anthropic devices. Anthropic *"could unilaterally approve or reject our requests"*, *"had unilateral redaction rights over all non-public information"*, and provided *"free tokens"*. METR *"did not take payment from Anthropic"*. METR didn't sample non-public models.
- **Not the embedded engagement.** METR says this *"is not the one described in Anthropic’s October 15 update on independent evaluators"* and wasn't meant to involve *"employee-like"* access. Business Insider's *"nearly three months inside Anthropic"* (via Digg) describes this report, not the 10-15 arrangement.
- **Why it might be wrong.** Too low if growth is speeding up further or automation is near. Too high if non-AI factors explain it, if the gains are limited to tasks that are easy to grade, or if Anthropic's earlier growth rate was unusually low (*"Anthropic models had a lower historical growth rate on AECI than the broader industry frontier before late 2025"*).
