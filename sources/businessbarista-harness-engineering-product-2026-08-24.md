## Summary

[[alex-lieberman|Alex Lieberman]] (Morning Brew co-founder, @businessbarista; AI-consulting practice) posts a **terminal-lifecycle prediction** for the harness discourse the wiki tracks: *"Agent harness & harness engineering will not be phrases uttered on the internet 12 months from now."* Not a dismissal — his claim is that the terms are **transitional scaffolding for applied AI's infancy** and will *"sit cleanly under 'product'"* once the complexity is abstracted out of the user's experience. The reply thread produces a materially larger claim from **[[garry-tan|Garry Tan]]** (YC CEO): *"systems of record will need to become AI harnesses or face replacement by agents."* Folds into [[loop-engineering]] (harness-layer thread; the standing **create-candidate `harness-engineering`** flag) and [[ai-native-organizations]] (Tan's systems-of-record claim). *(X posts; Lieberman's is a forward prediction, Tan's a reply-thread prediction.)*

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Lieberman — "harness engineering" is transitional vocabulary** (verbatim): *"Agent harness & harness engineering will not be phrases uttered on the internet 12 months from now. They're necessary for now as applied AI is in its infancy and complexity still hasn't been abstracted out of the user's experience, but this will all sit cleanly under 'product'."*
  - The register-lifecycle claim: prompt-engineering → context-engineering → harness/loop/graph-engineering are **practitioner-content registers naming a moving abstraction boundary**; as the boundary stabilizes, the naming collapses into "product." Pairs with the wiki's own note that *"the word may not survive the year; the design question will"* ([[graph-engineering]]).
- **Garry Tan — "systems of record will need to become AI harnesses or face replacement by agents"**: the enterprise-software restatement of harness-primacy. The harness thesis leaves coding and lands on **enterprise architecture**: the CRM/ERP/ticketing system either exposes itself as a governed context+permission surface an agent can drive end-to-end, or an agent-native competitor eats it. Extends the [[ai-native-organizations|"systems of record become the substrate"]] thread and Tan's own *"a markdown file is an employee"* / 2027 *"harness wars"* framing.
- **Eric Rea (Podium CEO) corroboration**: *"We started building agents and quickly realized we needed to build the system of record too. Agents need all the context and permissions you'd give an employee to complete work end-to-end. It goes both ways: the system of record makes the agent powerful, and agents [make the system of record valuable]."* — a builder-side receipt for Tan's claim: the agent and the system-of-record co-require each other.
- **Sushanth Raman counter-anchor**: *"Yet somehow, AS/400 systems have survived every era of computing"* — the incumbency/inertia counter to the replace-the-system-of-record prediction. Worth holding: systems of record are the *stickiest* enterprise software; "become a harness or die" may resolve as "slowly wrap a harness around 40-year-old cores."
- **"Godmother of Harness Engineering" reference** (@0xDepressionn, promotional): points at a 42-minute talk — *"what belongs in the context, the security holes nobody checks, and the harness you build around a model that is never going to be deterministic… One developer, $380 a day in tokens."* Rhymes with the [[loop-engineering|one-operator-high-token-spend]] receipts (0xMovez, Cherny) and the [[ai-vulnerability-discovery|security-holes-nobody-checks]] thread; entity unnamed, not promoted.
