## Summary

Anthropic's article (2026-11-10), linked from the @claudeai launch post saved in `_raw`. It adds three new artifact types to Claude: Sheets, Whiteboards and Data (renamed from Dashboards). Partner apps can now run inside the Claude window through interactive connectors. Bloomberg's framing in the 2026-11-10 brief was that the tools "take on its own backers". That's Bloomberg's angle; the article doesn't say it.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The pitch is Claude Code's pattern for everyone else.** *"Our goal is to bring that magic to every team"*. Engineers went from writing code to *"describing ideas and reviewing what Claude built."*
- **Sheets.** *"your team and Claude build spreadsheets together, collaboratively organize data, create and edit formulas, and work across multiple tabs."* Beta on paid plans, per the X post.
- **Whiteboards.** A shared board for a meeting, after which you *"ask Claude to summarize the findings."* Beta.
- **Data.** *"Claude Data (formerly Dashboards) connects to tools like Snowflake and Salesforce"*. It builds dashboards, data stories and narrated videos, and can use query definitions the data team certifies (beta). Generally available on paid plans, per the X post.
- **These join existing artifact types:** documents, slide decks, designs and Claude Motion animations (beta). Context carries across them, so an idea can move *"from a whiteboard to a spreadsheet to a dashboard"*.
- **Interactive connectors.** *"partners can embed their apps directly in Claude"*, starting with Adobe, Atlassian and Box. Plugins from Salesforce (beta), NetSuite and Workday (beta) come with built-in skills.
- **Connector count.** *"Claude Marketplace lists more than 9,000 connectors and plugins, including community-built ones."*
- **Admin controls.** Claude auto-connect (beta, Team and Enterprise) makes connecting an app a one-time setup for the whole organization. Admins can limit a connector so *"Claude might be able to read Slack but not send messages on your behalf."* On Enterprise, *"each cloud session runs in its own isolated virtual machine"*, with connector credentials kept outside it.
- **Customer quote** (Datadog CPO): product managers go *"from an idea to a feature brief, PRD, and testable prototype much faster"*. Vendor-selected.
