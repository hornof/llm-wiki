## Summary

Latent Space podcast (Basil Chatha, 2026-11-19, 84 minutes) with Linear's COO Cristina Cordova, read from the episode page's written summary rather than the transcript. Agents now create more than half of the issues in Linear, up from 3% a year earlier, and Linear passed $100M ARR without ever hiring an SDR. The most useful part for the wiki is her caution about the "AI budget" that is speeding up purchases, including Linear's.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Agents write most of the work:** *"Agents created 3% of Linear's work a year ago. Now it's more than half."* Linear shipped its MCP server in May 2025 (*"Linear shipped its MCP server in May 2025"*), and later an agents platform, integrations with Codex, Cursor and GitHub Copilot, and its own Linear Agent.
- **Numbers:** *"Linear passed $100 million in ARR this year with more than 40,000 paying customers and 177% net revenue retention"*; in August employees sold $99M of stock (*"employees sold $99 million in stock at a $2.5 billion valuation"*). When Cordova joined in 2023 it had *"about 40 people and one salesperson"*.
- **No SDRs:** *"Linear has never hired a single SDR."* She thinks the qualification call wastes the buyer's time; they want a demo.
- **Support stays human:** *"Linear won't let an LLM answer tickets on its own."* AI drafts replies and a person decides what goes out.
- **The AI budget, and its catch:** companies *"carved out money for AI tools that didn't exist before, and any product that fits inside it skips the usual fight for spend."* But buyers are *"signing one-year deals and paid pilots, not three-year contracts"*, and *"some overnight-$100M stories are a handful of $30 to $40 million contracts with defense companies or giant law firms."* The page's own line: *"When those one-year deals come up for renewal, we'll find out whose ARR was real."*
- **Enterprise plan dropped:** Linear declared an enterprise push in 2025, then agents arrived and *"a lot of the enterprise work never got done."*
