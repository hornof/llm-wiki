## Summary

Digg's summary, 2026-11-02, of SemiAnalysis's launch of GDPx, a benchmark for AI agents across what it calls the most valuable domains. The best model scores 12%. SemiAnalysis says safety classifiers drag down Opus 5.5's and GPT-6 Astra's overall scores in cyber, biology and alignment tasks. The SemiAnalysis post itself (newsletter.semianalysis.com, "GDPx: Measuring Frontier Model Capabilities Across the Most Valuable Domains") wasn't fetched, so the tasks, scoring and which model leads aren't known here.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Headroom:** SemiAnalysis *"put the top model’s score at 12%."* (Digg)
- **Refusals count against models:** it *"argued that safety classifiers depress the overall scores of Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra"*, in cyber, biology and alignment; alternative results without those domains exist but weren't in the post's text.
- **Google's reaction:** Sundar Pichai praised the benchmark's *"headroom"* and pointed to a cost line for Gemini 4 Carbon, without a figure.
- **Why it matters for evals:** a benchmark that scores a refusal as a failure measures the safety layer as much as the model. Any GDPx ranking needs the with- and without-classifier numbers side by side.
