## Summary

Simon Willison's link post on history professor Mark Humphries' account of GPT-6.2 Astra identifying the artist of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook at Yale's Beinecke Library. Humphries' own post wasn't fetched; quotes below are as Willison excerpts them.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The result** (Humphries via Willison): *"With only minimal prompting, GPT-6.2 Astra was able to autonomously identify the creator of an anonymous 1808 sketchbook"*, *"in just 22 minutes"*, using public sources. It found a little-known source, predicted it would confirm the hypothesis, and it did.
- **How** (Willison): Astra found a diary reference to a visit by the vicomte de Chabot *"dated five days before a date mentioned in the sketchbook"*, and suggested Chabot's travel journal, held at Cleveland State University, would confirm it.
- **Humphries' expectation**: historians will face *"a sort of crisis of confidence"* *"sometime next spring or summer"*, milder than mathematics', because historians *"don’t chase a single universal truth"*.
- AINews describes it as discovery *"beyond verifiable domains"* ([[ainews-gemini-4-neon-frontier-return-2026-11-24]]).
