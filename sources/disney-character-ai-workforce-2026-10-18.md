## Summary

TechCrunch "In Brief" item by Anthony Ha (2026-10-18). Character.AI filed for bankruptcy earlier the same week (TechCrunch's linked piece is dated 2026-10-13). Disney is the stalking-horse bidder for some of its assets at $20 million and has agreed to make offers to at least 60 employees, described as the majority of the workforce, with estimated annual compensation of $60 million. Former Character.AI CEO Karandeep Anand recently became Disney's first CTO.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Deal:** an asset purchase for $20 million, filed as a "stalking horse bid," so another buyer can still top it.
- **People:** offers to at least 60 employees; the filing says the "vast majority" accepted. Estimated annual compensation $60 million.
- **Why it failed, per the filing:** a "large and engaged user base," hurt by high costs and "potential litigation-related liabilities and regulatory engagement." The company faces a wrongful-death lawsuit over a 14-year-old user and government child-safety investigations, including one by the Texas attorney general.
- **Not in the article:** what happens to the product and its users, and any earlier Google arrangement.
