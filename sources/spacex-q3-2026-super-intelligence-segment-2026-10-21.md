## Summary

A Digg page collecting CNBC, FT, Verge and Techmeme reporting on SpaceX's Q3 2026 results. Revenue was **$14.58B, up 161% year on year**. The AI segment, which SpaceX calls **"Super Intelligence,"** brought in **$7.56B** and passed connectivity (Starlink, $5.25B) for the first time in the reporting. About $16.4B of $19.6B in Q3 capital spending went to AI.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Revenue:** $14.58B, against a $12.72B LSEG consensus (CNBC). **Net profit:** $4.4B, against a $1.1B loss a year earlier (FT).
- **AI segment ("Super Intelligence"):** $7.56B, up from $813M a year earlier. Operating income $431M, against a $1.7B loss a year earlier (Techmeme).
- **Why it grew** (The Verge): new cloud services agreements, the [[anysphere|Cursor]] acquisition, and more enterprise and consumer use of Grok. The segment's scope isn't defined beyond that.
- **Connectivity:** $5.25B, up from $3.1B; operating income $2.37B. Starlink subscribers 13.6M.
- **Capex:** $19.6B in Q3, of which more than $16.4B was AI (CNBC). The FT gives $18.4B for Q2.
- **Gaps:** AI plus connectivity is $12.81B, not $14.58B; the remaining ~$1.77B isn't explained. All figures are secondhand; no filing is cited.
