## Summary

**Allie K. Miller** with [[greg-isenberg|Greg Isenberg]] and Sahil Bloom on *The Startup Ideas Podcast*, 48 minutes, published **2026-08-12**. Miller describes running **a workforce of 34 AI agents** with an explicit management hierarchy — the most concrete single-operator instance of [[ai-native-organizations]] the wiki holds.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

### The org chart

- **34 AI agents**, overseen by an **AI chief of staff named "Simon"**, plus **six director-level agents** (reportedly named after *Friends* characters).
- **Miller sits three levels above her agents.** That is the striking part: not a human directing a pool of tools, but a human at the top of a **multi-layer hierarchy of agents**, with middle management that is itself agentic.

This is the [[block-organizational-intelligence|Block/Sequoia "from hierarchy to intelligence"]] thesis inverted in an interesting way. Block argues AI *dissolves* the span-of-control constraint that forced hierarchy. Miller **rebuilds the hierarchy out of agents** — same layers, same routing function, non-human occupants. Whether that is a transitional shape or the durable one is the open question, and it is a sharper version of it than the wiki has had.

### The prompt and the context

- **"Do smart things"** — her three-word core prompt, resting on extensive accumulated business context. The brevity is the claim: when context is rich enough, instruction can be thin.
- **Daily dictated diaries**, to preserve context that never makes it into formal channels. The [[company-brain|memory-substrate]] problem solved by narrating into it, and a direct answer to the *"getting signals in"* function.

### Control mechanisms

- **"AI watchdogs"** that flag **duplicate work and scheduling conflicts** — the failure mode you would predict from 34 agents with overlapping remits, handled by more agents rather than by coordination overhead. Compare [[croovies-loop-orchestrator-mission-note-2026-09-10|croovies's orchestrator]], which does this with a supervisor plus a SQLite ticket table.

### Strategy

- **"Build the factory before the product"** — reusable primitives and infrastructure first, so later launches accelerate. The [[domain-specific-harness|harness-as-the-asset]] argument stated as a sequencing rule.
- On market shape: **enterprises seek vendor relationships**, while **consumer software increasingly rewards design taste and distribution**.
