## Summary

A manifesto written by speakers and attendees of the AI Engineer Code Summit 2026 in San Francisco and introduced in the closing keynote. It's modeled on the 2001 Agile Manifesto: two values, a diagnosis of why slop happens, and a list of resolutions. [[swyx]] is among the credited contributors, and Latent Space ran it as the title story of AINews on 2026-11-13 ([[ainews-no-ai-slop-llamacpp-langchain-2026-11-13]]). Signatures are collected through gist comments. It reads as a team norm for AI-assisted work, which is also what the wiki's own style guide and filler lint try to be.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The two values:** *"Our own thinking over a model's first draft"* and *"Improving the system over patching the output"*.
- **The definition:** *"Slop is when we put less effort into producing something than we ask the consumer to spend understanding it."* And: *"Using AI doesn't necessarily make something slop."*
- **Why it spreads:** *"Slop is asymmetric. The cost of producing a thousand-line PR or a ten-page doc is approaching zero, while the cost of reviewing it remains constant."* *"Slop compounds. The more slop sits in our knowledge base, in chat, in code, the more of it the next agent reads and copies."*
- **Disclosure and review:** *"When an agent wrote something we're sending, we make that clear, and we check the parts that matter before it goes out."* *"Agent reviews, linters, and verifiers are useful but not sufficient."* How much to review *"depends on the stakes."*
- **Fix the system:** *"When a mistake shows up twice, we scale ourselves and our team by improving the system and its guardrails."*
- **Metrics:** what shipped and who uses it *"matters FAR MORE than lines of code, prs merged, tokens spent, or the number of agents running in parallel."*
- **Not absolutist:** *"One-shot prompts and vibe coding are high leverage tools."* *"None of us has this figured out."*
- **For the owner:** "slop compounds in the knowledge base" is the reason the wiki's lint has a filler rule, and "when a mistake shows up twice, improve the system" is how CLAUDE.md has grown. The manifesto could be cited as the outside statement of both. [Judgment.]
