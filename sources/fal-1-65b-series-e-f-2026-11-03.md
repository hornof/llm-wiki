## Summary

Sourcery (Molly O'Shea, 2026-11-03) article and interview with fal's co-founders, Burkay Gür (CEO) and Görkem Yurtseven (CTO), on a combined $1.65B Series E and F. fal runs inference for image, video, audio and 3D models. Most of the piece is the founders' view of generative media as a market and how they run a ~150-person company with two reporting levels. Founder claims throughout; no financials beyond the round.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The round.** *"fal has raised $1.65B in a combined Series E and Series F. Sequoia led the Series E and Bessemer Venture Partners led the Series F."* Valuation not stated. Prior: *"Sequoia also led fal’s $140M Series D at a $4.5B valuation in December 2025"*.
- **Three product-market fits.** Gür: *"AI really has 3 product market fits. One of them is search. The other really clear one that’s growing really fast now is coding agents. And the third one is, we believe, generative media."* Coding and generative media are where *"an individual user spends $10K to $20K a month"*.
- **Revenue mix.** *"Video grew from 0 to roughly 70% of fal’s revenue after video models arrived."* Image editing models pulled image back up, *"and the split is now 50/50"*.
- **Higher prices, more demand.** *"Google’s Veo 2 launched at $5 per 5-second clip"*, against roughly 30–40 cents for early Kling and MiniMax models. Yurtseven: *"Turns out there is essentially infinite demand."*
- **Org design with coding agents.** *"fal has at most 2 levels of reporting and no pure managers."* Leaders are "super ICs" who build and lead. *"Yurtseven estimates coding agents make fal’s engineering team 3 to 4X faster, meaning fal would need roughly 4 times the headcount without them."* Gür expects each technical leader to supervise *"5 to 10 engineers"*. Crunch periods mean *"60- to 80-hour weeks"*.
- **Compute shortage.** Labs now need inference capacity before launch: *"If you are coming up with a new model, you really have to plan ahead of time."* fal procures ahead so capacity *"does not go entirely to coding agents"*.
- **Hollywood.** Gür: *"So far I would say AI had zero impact on anything in Hollywood."* Hollywood revenue has grown over six months; studios want open models they can fine-tune.
- **Margins.** The episode's stated thesis is that margin sits with compute providers rather than applications (timestamp title *"Why the application layer has no margins"*).
