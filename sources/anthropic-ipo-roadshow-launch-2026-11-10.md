## Summary

Anthropic's Rule 134-style notice (2026-11-10) launching the IPO roadshow, two weeks after the public S-1 ([[anthropic-s1-public-filing-2026-10-26]]). It gives the share count, the price range and the ticker. It does not give a valuation or the total shares outstanding, so the market value implied by the range can't be worked out from this page.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Size.** *"The proposed offering consists of 825,000,000 shares of Class A common stock."* Of these, *"801,351,354 shares are being offered by Anthropic"* and *"23,648,646 shares are being offered by certain existing stockholders"*. Underwriters get a 30-day option on *"up to an additional 123,750,000 shares"*.
- **Price.** *"between $70.00 and $78.00 per share"*. By this page's arithmetic, Anthropic's own 801.35M shares would raise about $56.1B at the low end and $62.5B at the high end, before the underwriters' option and fees.
- **Listing.** Nasdaq Global Select Market *"under the symbol “ANT.”"*
- **Bookrunners named for the prospectus:** Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley.
- **Not stated:** valuation, shares outstanding, pricing date. The valuation estimates the wiki holds ($1.1T low end, $2T earlier expectation) are press reports; see [[openai-11b-raise-anthropic-ipo-valuation-2026-11-06]].
- **Timing.** Fable 5.2 shipped the day before ([[anthropic-claude-fable-mythos-5-2-2026-11-09]]). The 2026-10-01 brief's reported date for IPO marketing, the week of 2026-11-09, was right.
