## Summary

GPT-6.1 Terra, OpenAI's new mid-tier model for computer use and everyday work, launched 2026-10-26. openai.com returned 403, so this page uses the Digg roundup, which quotes OpenAI's and ChatGPT's own X posts and Artificial Analysis's benchmark post. **The prices come from Artificial Analysis, not from OpenAI's text.** It's the first Terra-tier model since GPT-5.6 and the successor to [[gpt-5-6|GPT-5.6 Terra]].

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **OpenAI:** *"GPT-6.1 Terra understands what’s on screen, takes action across apps, writes naturally"*.
- **Availability (ChatGPT's post):** *"rolling out today to Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex."* The API is also available (Digg summary).
- **Price (Artificial Analysis):** *"$1/$5 per million input/output tokens vs $2/$12 for the previous model"*; cached input *"$0.05 vs $0.20 per million tokens"*.
- **Benchmark (Artificial Analysis):** at max effort, *"GPT-6.1 Terra gains 6 points in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index over GPT-5.6 Terra, at less than a third of the cost per task ($0.40 vs $1.40)"*.
- **Claims not used:** a user post (via Digg's summary) says it matches GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam at a tenth of the price, and is three times faster. That's a secondhand relay of an OpenAI claim not captured here.
