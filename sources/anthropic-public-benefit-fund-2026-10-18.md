## Summary

TechCrunch "In Brief" item by Anthony Ha (2026-10-18) on an Anthropic announcement: a "public benefit fund," set up as a wholly owned entity inside Anthropic, funded with 5% of the company's shares plus 2% more for every 50% rise in the company's value. Uses named are large donations to nonprofits, direct distributions to specific populations, and work on AI's effect on jobs. Structure and governance are to be described "in the coming months."

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Size:** 5% of shares up front; an extra 2% for each 50% increase in company value. No dollar value given.
- **Form:** a wholly owned entity within Anthropic. The article does not say who controls it, who picks the projects, or whether it holds any voting or board rights.
- **Uses:** large donations to traditional nonprofits; direct distributions to specific populations; AI and jobs, where Anthropic says it has already committed $200 million to study the issue. On bolder approaches: *"The Fund will have the versatility to pursue them."*
- **Context given:** growing negative public sentiment toward AI. TechCrunch recalls [[dario-amodei|Amodei]] calling the backlash *"fundamentally a crisis of trust."*
- **Not in the article:** any link to the IPO, the Long-Term Benefit Trust, or the founders' voting-control arrangement of 2026-09-25.
