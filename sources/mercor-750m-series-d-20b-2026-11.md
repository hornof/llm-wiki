## Summary

Sourcery post (2026-11-10) on Mercor's Series D. Mercor started as AI-powered recruiting and now sells training data, evals and reinforcement-learning environments built from contractor work. The round doubles its valuation in about a year. Revenue is described as a growth multiple, with no dollar figure.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Round.** *"Mercor has raised a $750M Series D at a $20B valuation."* It included an employee tender. Investors include Nvidia, Benchmark, General Catalyst, Felicis, Apollo affiliates, Salesforce and XTX Markets.
- **Prior mark.** *"The new mark doubles Mercor’s last priced round."* The Series C was *"a $350M Series C at a $10B valuation on October 27, 2025"*.
- **Revenue.** *"Revenue has grown more than 5X since September 2025."* No absolute figure.
- **Customers widen.** Now *"beyond frontier labs to big tech, open model developers, application layer companies and the Fortune 500."*
- **Thesis, quoted in the post:** *"Every Fortune 500 is going to look like a Neolab"*, curating tasks for evaluating and training its own models.
- **Same-week context.** Latent Space reports that Handshake's AI-training business *"went from launch to $1 billion in gross annualized revenue"* in roughly a year (https://latent.space/p/handshake/, not otherwise ingested). Two data vendors with large numbers in one week support the view that expert-labelled data is something labs buy rather than build. See [[data-as-moat-frontier-2026-05-30]].
