## Summary

The Information (2026-10-31, via Digg) reports that DeepSeek doubled its latest fundraising target as black-market prices for Nvidia's advanced chips rose, with the round nearing $16B. Backers include Tencent, CATL and Chinese state-owned institutions. This extends the 10-23 Bloomberg report of a roughly $15B raise ([[deepseek-15b-raise-2026-10-23]]).

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Size:** *"The round reportedly approached $16 billion"*, after DeepSeek *"doubled its latest fundraising target"*.
- **Backers:** Tencent, CATL and *"some of China’s most powerful state-owned institutions"*. Amounts per backer weren't given.
- **The chip-price link is The Information's account**, not shown with numbers in the summary.
