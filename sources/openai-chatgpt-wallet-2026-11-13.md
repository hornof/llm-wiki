## Summary

Digg's summary (17 sources, CNBC featured) of ChatGPT Wallet, which [[openai|OpenAI]] announced on 2026-11-13. Its Dots agents can now buy things, but the user approves every purchase, and the agent gets a network payment token rather than the card number. The launch is limited to US Pro subscribers. Hands-off spending within preset limits is a stated goal with no date. OpenAI's own post wasn't fetched.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **What and who:** OpenAI *"announced ChatGPT Wallet on November 13, 2026, beginning a limited rollout through its dots agents."* *"The initial rollout is for U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and older with eligible U.S.-issued cards. OpenAI built Wallet with American Express, Mastercard and Visa."*
- **How it pays:** the user reviews *"the items, total and selected card"*, then approves *"with a security check, such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan."* *"After approval, the payment network supplies a payment token"*, so the card is charged *"without sharing its actual details with the agent or business."*
- **Revenue:** *"OpenAI told CNBC it isn’t taking a cut of transactions at launch."*
- **Known failure modes:** *"some sites may unintentionally block Wallet purchases, while payment-risk systems may flag legitimate agent transactions, causing declines or cancellations."* OpenAI published a *"Commerce Partner Playbook"* for merchants.
- **Next step, undated:** *"OpenAI expects users eventually to be able to pre-approve purchases within parameters such as spending limits, purchase categories or time periods"*. *"It hasn’t supplied a delivery date."*
- **For the owner:** the design is the agent-permission pattern again: a scoped credential issued per action, with a human approving each one. The next step OpenAI describes is standing permissions within limits, the same move Claude Meetings makes for connected tools, where one yes covers later uses ([[anthropic-claude-meetings-beta-2026-11-12]]).
