## Summary

Anthropic's public S-1, filed with the SEC on 2026-10-26 (CIK 0002130357, accession 0001628280-26-067168), read from the filing itself, with two TechCrunch pieces by Rebecca Bellan from the same day ([debut](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-officially-files-for-its-public-debut/), [enterprise strategy](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropics-ipo-filing-reveals-its-strategy-for-keeping-enterprise-customers-hooked/)). **This is the first frontier-lab prospectus the wiki has read in full text rather than through press reports or a leak.** It confirms the leaked H1 figures of 2026-10-20, shows Anthropic **operating-profitable in Q2 2026**, and answers several long-open questions on [[anthropic]]. The offering size, price range and latest run-rate figure are still blank in this draft.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

All quotes are from the S-1 unless marked TechCrunch.

- **Listing:** Nasdaq Global Select Market, ticker ANT (*"under the symbol “ANT.”"*). Price range blank (*"between $ and $"*).
- **Revenue:**
  - H1 2026: $16.3B, up from $1.2B (*"Our revenue was $16.3 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2026, up from $1.2 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2025"*).
  - Q2 2026: $11.6B (*"Our revenue was $11.6 billion for the three months ended June 30, 2026, up from $787 million for the three months ended June 30, 2025"*). So Q1 2026 was about $4.7B (my subtraction).
  - Full years 2023–2025: $41M, $386M, $4.6B (*"our revenue was $41 million, $386 million, and $4.6 billion, respectively"*).
  - The filing warns this pace won't last: *"We do not expect our revenue will continue to grow at comparable rates"*.
- **Profitability:**
  - Q2 2026 was operating-profitable: *"Our operating margin was 8% and operating income was $1.0 billion for the three months ended June 30, 2026"*. Adjusted: *"an Adjusted Operating Margin of 19% and Adjusted Operating Income of $2.2 billion"*.
  - H1 2026 operating loss $693M (*"Our operating loss was $693 million for the six months ended June 30, 2026"*).
  - H1 2026 net loss $80.7B (*"$80.7 billion for the six months ended June 30, 2026"*). The income statement puts **$72.4B of it in one line**, the change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities and SAFEs (table row: *"(72,388)"*, in millions). It's an accounting charge from earlier financings, not cash burn.
- **Gross margin, implied:** the filing no longer reports gross profit (*"cost of revenue, gross profit (loss), research and development, sales and marketing, and general and administrative are no longer presented as separate line items"*). A note still breaks out cost of revenue: **$2,380M in H1 2026 against $16,286M revenue** (table values *"$ 2,380"* and *"$ 16,286"*), which is mostly the compute that serves customers (*"compute and infrastructure costs associated with customer-serving workloads"*). That implies a gross margin of about 85% (my arithmetic). Training compute sits in R&D ($9,741M), so this is a serving margin, not an all-in one.
- **Customers** (as of 2026-09-30, trailing twelve months):
  - *"More than 11,900 customers with $100,000 or more of revenue on a TTM basis"*, up *"from more than 950"* a year earlier.
  - *"More than 1,900 customers with $1 million or more"*.
  - *"Approximately 250 customers with $10 million or more"*.
  - Net revenue retention about 900%: *"Net Revenue Retention of approximately 900% as of September 30, 2026"*.
  - Cross-sell (TechCrunch's wording; I didn't find the exact sentence in the filing text): more than half of $100K+ customers use both the platform and a first-party app, *"up from about 30% at the end of last year"*.
  - Claude Code spend: *"Claude Enterprise users who did not use Claude Code spent less than $75 per month on average, while the top 5% of Claude Enterprise users who used Claude Code spent approximately 20 times that amount"*.
- **Customer concentration:** one customer was 11% of H1 2026 revenue. In 2025, two customers were 12% each, which matches the leak's figure of about a fifth of sales (revenue table: *"Customer A * * 12% * * Customer B * * 12% 18% * Customer C 43% * * * 11%"*). None is named.
- **Run-rate revenue is now defined:** *"the sum of (i) our consumption-based fees during the trailing 28-day period ended on the measurement date, multiplied by 13, and (ii) our aggregate monthly subscription fees as of the measurement date, multiplied by 12"*. The latest figure is blank (*"Run-Rate Revenue was $ as of October 31, 2026"*).
- **Compute commitments named in the filing:**
  - SpaceXAI (formerly X.AI): *"we agreed to spend an aggregate of up to $120.5 billion over terms extending into 2029, consisting of approximately $3.1 billion in the aggregate per month once all capacity has been delivered"*.
  - Microsoft: *"we committed to spend an aggregate of at least $44.9 billion between November 2026 and May 2033, regardless of actual usage"*.
  - A 2026 lease arrangement tied to Broadcom capacity: *"approximately $125.2 billion excluding interest"*.
  - After 2026-06-30: new and amended agreements with *"aggregate future minimum payment obligations of approximately $245 billion"*, running up to 20 years.
  - The headline total is blank (*"an aggregate committed spend of more than $"*).
- **Funding rounds:**
  - Series G-1: *"From January 2026 to February 2026, we sold an aggregate of 90,510,425 shares of our Series G-1 redeemable convertible preferred stock at a purchase price of $259.1364 per share"*.
  - Series H-1: *"From May 2026 to June 2026"*, at *"$589.0095 per share, for an aggregate purchase price of $45,729,447,703"*. Series H-2 at the same price adds about $19.5B (table: *"Series H-2 34,808 33,110 $ 589.01 19,502 19,502"*), so H-1 plus H-2 is about $65.2B (my sum).
- **Governance:**
  - Founder LLC: the seven co-founders hold one Class F share with *"votes that equals 50.1% of the total voting power"*.
  - Long-Term Benefit Trust: through Class T stock it elects *"the Class T directors, who together will constitute a majority of the authorized members of our board of directors"*.
  - Public Benefit Fund: *"we contributed 846,840,960 shares of our Class N common stock, representing 5 % of our fully diluted shares as of June 30, 2026"*.
  - Employees get a Class E share class *"entitled to vote only on certain limited matters"*.
- **Risks worth tracking:**
  - FTC: *"In October 2026, the FTC issued a civil investigative demand to us seeking information about our consumer protection, data protection, and privacy practices, as well as the safety and security of our products"*.
  - Agentic cost: *"the aggregate compute required to serve our customers could increase disproportionately relative to the associated revenue"*.
  - Safety language: advanced AI *"could, if not developed, deployed, and operated with appropriate safeguards, oversight, and security measures, pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity"*.
  - Mythos gating cited as a precedent: *"in April 2026, we initially released our frontier model, Mythos, as a gated research preview through Project Glasswing"*.
  - Cloud partners as competitors (TechCrunch's summary): Amazon, Google and Microsoft *"may prioritize their own requirements or rivals they’ve invested in"*.

## What this settles on the wiki

- **The 10-20 leak was accurate** on $16.3B revenue and the $80.7B net loss. Its open question, how much of the loss was a financing adjustment, has an answer: $72.4B.
- **Run-rate methodology** (open on [[anthropic]] since 2026-05-29): defined above. It annualises a 28-day window, so it runs ahead of recognised revenue in a fast-growth quarter.
- **Series G date:** January to February 2026. The 2026-05-30 brief's date of February 2024 was wrong.
- **Series H size:** about $65.2B across H-1 and H-2, matching the wiki's $65B.
- **Not settled:** the Spark $75M → $3B return (Spark appears only as a G-1 buyer of about $25M), and the Q3 revenue figure of $16.8B reported 2026-10-15 (the filing's statements stop at June 30).
