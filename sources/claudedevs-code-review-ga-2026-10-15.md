## Summary

@ClaudeDevs announcement (2026-10-15): [[claude-code|Claude Code]] **Code Review is generally available on Team and Enterprise**, five months after it was announced at Code w/ Claude (2026-05-06). Admins enable it in Claude Code settings, pick repos, and set a review depth per repo. After that it runs on every new PR with no developer setup.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Three review levels**, chosen by admins per repo: Quick, Standard, Deep.
- **~3× faster** than during the research preview for most reviews.
- Findings are "concise" and state **the problem, the trigger and the fix**.
- **Promotion**: eligible Enterprise orgs can claim at least $10,000 in Code Review credits by "Jan 15" (no year given; 2027-01-15 from context). Credits expire 30 days after claiming.
- **Plan scope**: Team and Enterprise only. The top reply asks why Max plans keep getting excluded from new features, so individual power users are left out.

*(Vendor announcement. "3× faster" and "more of the bugs that matter" are unbenchmarked vendor claims. The internal numbers behind them are in [[claude-code-team-code-review-2026-10-15]].)*
