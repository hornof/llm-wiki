## Summary

Anthropic post (2026-10-15, footnotes clarified 10-15 and 10-16) saying it has given a [[metr|METR]] team **employee-like access** inside the company, and describing a three-tier framework for working with outside evaluators. It presents this as following from Anthropic's signing of the **White House Accord on Super Intelligence**, in which companies pledged to work with independent external auditors or evaluators. Surfaced by `Daily Briefs/2026-10-17.md`; primary fetched.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **METR (Tier 3)**: six METR staff get company hardware, office space, and internal tools and systems "roughly on par" with Anthropic's own risk-assessment teams. They are encouraged to share information, with exceptions for defined categories (customer data, privileged information, model architectures). **METR will publish its analysis. "Anthropic can't veto publication, but we can require them to redact information"** in the predefined categories.
- **Tier 1**: narrowly scoped risk investigations. Anthropic controls access, and publication needs its approval.
- **Tier 2**: more information, and publication without approval, subject to category redactions.
- **Other named partners**: **SecureBio** is reviewing the chem/bio chapter of the August Risk Report, after a pilot on the previous one. **Accenture and Faculty** are pursuing a broader partnership covering evaluation and red-teaming, alignment assessments, safeguard testing and capability building. **[[hugging-face|Hugging Face]]** is in early discussions, along with "several other organizations."
- *"We're embedding evaluators voluntarily, and we believe every frontier AI company should do the same."*
- *"It's not a way for other organizations to obtain our information, or that of our customers and partners."*
- **Not stated**: UK AISI or US CAISI involvement, funding (including whether METR is paid), pre-deployment timing, weights access, risk thresholds, or how these terms differ from earlier arrangements beyond the tier structure.

*(Vendor post describing its own governance arrangement. The terms are Anthropic's account, with no METR statement captured.)*
