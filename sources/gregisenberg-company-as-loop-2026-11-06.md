## Summary

A five-line post from [[greg-isenberg|Greg Isenberg]] (2026-11-06) describing company-building as a loop: acquire context, orchestrate agents on it, feed data back, *"call humans like an api"*, repeat. Replies are mostly jokes; two make substantive objections.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The loop.** *"acquire valuable context"*, *"orchestrate agents to build on it"*, *"feed the data back in"*, *"call humans like an api"*, *"repeat"*. Closing line: *"the best context/faster loop wins"*.
- **Objection: data contamination.** @jeffbruchado: *"feed the data back in gets weird once half the rows are stuff an agent wrote two loops ago"*. This is the error-propagation risk the wiki tracks under [[llm-wiki-pattern]].
- **Objection: moats.** @vishalsingh2972: *"then what moat is left?"* Unanswered in the thread.
- **Practitioner aside.** @_yukungao: *"~/.codex alone is sitting at 22 GB on this Mac."*
- The framing matches [[loop-engineering]] and [[context-engineering]] applied to a whole company rather than a single agent. No evidence or numbers attached; it is a thesis post.
