## Summary

Google DeepMind's announcement (Raia Hadsell, Diego Rivas, Hang Qi; 2026-10-27) of Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model. A user steers the scene with keyboard controls and text prompts. It generates sound and dialogue along with the video. Gemini Omni anchors appearance and behavior, and other Gemini models keep the simulation coherent. The release is a research preview for trusted testers. Google AI Ultra subscribers and Gemini API early-access partners come next. **Vendor post; no independent evaluation yet.** The page publishes no resolution, frame rate or session length.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Native audio and dialogue:** *"Genie 4 is our first real-time world model to generate audio natively"*, and characters *"converse and react to your inputs or events in the world, all in real-time"*.
- **Built on Gemini:** *"Gemini Omni anchors the simulations in real-world appearance and behavior, while other Gemini models provide the complex reasoning needed to help maintain a simulation’s coherence and continuity."* Between user inputs, *"Gemini also monitors and steers the scene"*.
- **Stated use:** *"developing training environments for robots and agents"*. DeepMind says it is *"experimenting with Gemini 4 Argon and Genie 4"* alongside Gemini Robotics, and enterprise partners are evaluating it for scenario generation.
- **Limitations, in DeepMind's words:** *"visual details and object permanence can still shift or drift over time"*, especially in long sessions or after many prompt edits. Physics, audio accuracy, legible text, spatial accuracy and *"long-term consistency are active areas of ongoing research"*.
- **Predecessor:** Genie 3, August 2025, per the related-posts list.
