## Summary

Nvidia's press release of 2026-11-23, read with Digg's summary of the coverage (NBC, FT, AFP), TechCrunch's report on Megaspeed the same day, and Digg on Arm CEO Rene Haas. The board unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as president, CEO and director, 11 days after he resigned ([[nvidia-ceo-transition-2026-11-12]]). The board says US officials told it his return poses no national security risk; it hasn't said who they were or what they said. The investigation into whether Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort continues.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Reinstated, unanimously** (Nvidia): *"effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board. The Board of Directors voted unanimously to reinstate Mr. Huang"*. Colette Kress goes back to being CFO only; Harvey Jones stays chair.
- **The security assurance** (Nvidia): the board *"received input from senior levels of the United States government, and based on that input, the Board is confident that Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk."* Digg, citing AFP: Nvidia didn't name the officials or say what they told the board, and the statement included no findings from the investigation.
- **A reversal within a week** (Digg citing NBC): on November 17 Jones said directors did *“not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO,”* and he returns *"11 days after his resignation"*. Digg and TechCrunch both say Trump pushed for the return; TechCrunch: *"After lobbying from President Trump, Jensen Huang was reinstated"*.
- **Investigation and compliance** (Nvidia): the independent directors' investigation *"will proceed to conclusion"*. A new National Security and Export Control Committee oversees trade compliance, and Nvidia is hiring a Chief Compliance Officer who reports to the board's governance committee.
- **Megaspeed heads for the Entity List** (TechCrunch citing Semafor, anonymous sources): Megaspeed, the Singapore cloud provider Alice Huang led *"for over two years"*, *"will be placed on a U.S. Commerce Department blacklist"*. It was one of Nvidia's biggest chip customers, and US and Singapore authorities are investigating whether it smuggled Nvidia chips to China. Megaspeed denies wrongdoing.
- **The successor race that didn't happen** (Digg citing Bloomberg via Kalshi and Techmeme): Arm CEO Rene Haas, approached by a recruiter about the Nvidia job, extended his Arm contract *"through 2033"* and becomes SoftBank vice chair on December 1.
- **Not verified here:** the brief's item that Huang is scheduled for a December 3 Senate hearing. Its Digg link returned the reinstatement story, and no fetched text mentions the date.
