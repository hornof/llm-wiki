## Summary

[[nvidia|NVIDIA]]'s board announced on 2026-11-12 that [[jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] has resigned as president and CEO and left the board, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and the board named its first chair. Nvidia's release gives no reason. Also read: TechCrunch's analysis (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/12/five-ways-jensen-huangs-ouster-from-nvidia-could-damage-the-ai-industry/), an opinion piece, and Digg's summary (https://digg.com/tech/8grl9dfi).

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The facts (Nvidia):** *"Jensen Huang has resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately."* Colette Kress, CFO since 2013, *"has agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer"* and stays CFO. The board *"will consider internal and external candidates."*
- **Governance change:** Harvey C. Jones, formerly CEO of Synopsys, becomes the board's *"first-ever Chair of the Board."*
- **Continuity message:** the board expects *"operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption."* Q3 fiscal 2027 results are still due *"on Tuesday, November 17"*.
- **Why he left (TechCrunch, not Nvidia):** the board ousted him after he admitted an affair. TechCrunch reports the other party is an executive at a Singapore-based neocloud that the US suspects of smuggling Nvidia chips to China. A second allegation involved an Nvidia employee (Digg). Republican committee chairs are summoning him to testify in closed sessions. The wiki records these only as the reported cause.
- **What the industry loses, by TechCrunch's account.** These are the writer's arguments:
  - Huang was *"the biggest, loudest advocate for open weight AI models"*.
  - He gathered *"over 100 companies"* behind Nvidia's Open Agent Safety Platform.
  - He drove the new AI-PC line and the financing behind the data-center buildout, including *"$750 billion"* in circular deals reported by Bloomberg.
- **Hugging Face hasn't closed.** TechCrunch: the $12.9B acquisition *"is supposed to close in the first half of next year, pending regulatory approval."*
- **Trump's suggestion:** he floated Apple's Tim Cook as a replacement on Truth Social. Axios, via Digg, says it's unclear whether he was joking.
