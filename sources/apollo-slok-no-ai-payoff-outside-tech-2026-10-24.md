## Summary

Torsten Slok, Apollo's chief economist, in The Daily Spark (2026-10-24): **revenue per worker and profit margins are rising for the Mag 7 and flat for the S&P 493,** and total factor productivity shows "still no signs of AI boosting productivity growth." His conclusion: *"the AI story will be decided in the S&P 493, not the Mag 7."*

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The numbers are in charts only.** The text gives the direction but no values. Revenue per worker is inflation-adjusted to 2019 Q4, with data from Bloomberg and Macrobond. TFP comes from the SF Fed and BLS.
- **AI spend is concentrated in a few firms,** and token spending is circular (both from Ramp data).
- *A macro read from a capital allocator, and a short note. It doesn't separate "no adoption" from "adoption without payoff yet."*
