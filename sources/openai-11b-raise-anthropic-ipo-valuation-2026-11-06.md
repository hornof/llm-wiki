## Summary

TechCrunch In Brief (Lucas Ropek, 2026-11-06) on a Bloomberg report: OpenAI has raised $11B toward a round valuing it at $1.25T, well short of the $30B target reported in September. The same piece relays Bloomberg's report that Anthropic IPO buyers are eyeing a valuation as low as $1.1T.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The raise.** *"OpenAI has reportedly raised $11 billion so far in a new funding round that values the AI lab at $1.25 trillion."* The round *"could total about $20 billion"*, per a Bloomberg source.
- **Short of target.** *"In late September, Bloomberg reported that OpenAI was looking to raise $30 billion in the upcoming round."*
- **Anthropic.** *"some potential buyers believe Anthropic’s valuation could be as low as $1.1 trillion, compared with the $2 trillion that investors were said to be expecting roughly a month ago."*
- **OpenAI IPO.** Delayed from this year; expected *"next year at some point"*.
- **Market backdrop.** TechCrunch ties the jitters to the midterm results and *"overwhelmingly negative voter sentiment around data centers"*.
- **Correction to the brief.** The 2026-11-07 brief called this round "at same valuation as Anthropic's IPO filing" and gave Anthropic a "$1.25T–$1.75T" range (Digg). Neither matches this article: the S-1 names no valuation, and Bloomberg's low-end buyer figure is $1.1T. Recorded as reported, not as fact.
