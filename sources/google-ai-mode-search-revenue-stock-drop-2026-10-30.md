## Summary

AINews (Latent Space, 2026-10-30) leads with Alphabet's earnings fallout. Google's chief business officer said AI Mode's growth weighed on Search because AI Mode queries earn less, and the stock fell about 15%, roughly $500B of market value. **The brief misread this as AI Mode costing $500B of Search revenue.** The $500B is market value, and Google gave no revenue figure for AI Mode.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Google's own words (Philipp Schindler, CBO, as quoted):** *"It’s worth noting that the strong growth in AI Mode queries had an impact because of the lower monetization we were seeing in AI Mode."*
- **No breakdown:** *"Google doesn’t break out AI Mode in sufficient detail that we can make any sort of rigorous analysis"*.
- **Market move:** headline only, *"-15% $500B drop worst since 2004 IPO"*. The share-price figures weren't checked against market data.
- **Context AINews adds:** souring AI sentiment in public markets, and OpenAI raising at $1.25T rather than $1.4T, ahead of Anthropic's IPO.
