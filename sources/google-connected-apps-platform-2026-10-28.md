## Summary

TechCrunch (Sarah Perez, 2026-10-28) on Google's Connected Apps Platform. It opens the third-party app integrations in Gemini and Search to any developer, who builds on MCP or Android AppFunctions and submits through a new console. It's in public preview for US developers on a waitlist. TechCrunch compares it to SEO: a way for businesses to be visible to AI agents.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **How it works:** developers *"integrate with a cloud-hosted MCP (Model Context Protocol) server or Android MCP using the AppFunctions APIs"*, then register and submit in the Google Connected Console. They choose Search, the Gemini app or both, and they can see traffic, latency and error rates.
- **Scale today:** *"over 50 Connected Apps available in Gemini"* (Adobe, Experian, Peloton and others), coming to Search *"over the next few weeks"*.
- **Availability:** *"public preview for U.S. developers who sign up for the waitlist"*. No general-availability date.
- **TechCrunch's framing:** the bet is that people stop opening apps and use them through their AI provider instead. ChatGPT and Meta's Muse have their own versions.
