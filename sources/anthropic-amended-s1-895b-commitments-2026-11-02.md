## Summary

Digg's summary, 2026-11-02, of reporting by CNBC, Yahoo Finance and CoinDesk on Anthropic's amended S-1, filed 2026-11-02. The amendment lists about $895.4B of purchase and lease commitments, nearly all for compute and infrastructure. These are future obligations, not money spent. It also adds third-quarter results: revenue near $17B, slower growth, and a return to an operating loss. The amended filing itself wasn't read.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The total:** the amended prospectus *"lists about $895.4 billion in purchase and lease commitments, substantially all tied to compute and infrastructure."*
- **Not spent:** *"These are future obligations, not money already spent."*
- **Timing:** *"$447.5 billion of the commitments is scheduled before Dec. 31, 2030, with the remainder falling between 2031 and 2045"* (Yahoo Finance); *"$69.8 billion is due within 12 months of Sept. 30, 2026"* (CoinDesk).
- **Q3:** *"Revenue reached nearly $17 billion for the quarter, compared with $33.24 billion for the nine months ended Sept. 30."*
- **Growth slowed:** *"Quarterly revenue grew about 47% from the second quarter, down from 144% growth in that earlier quarter."*
- **Margin:** *"higher costs pushed the company back to an operating loss."* Q2 had been operating-profitable ([[anthropic-s1-public-filing-2026-10-26]]).
- **Against earlier figures:** the leaked S-1 gave $450B–$750B ([[anthropic-s1-leak-h1-loss-compute-2026-10-20]]). $447.5B through 2030 matches the leak's $450B of cloud agreements; the extra comes from commitments running to 2045.
