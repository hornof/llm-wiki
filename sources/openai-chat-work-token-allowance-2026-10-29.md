## Summary

Digg's summary, first seen around 2026-10-29, of a subscriber email reported by BleepingComputer and TechCrunch. OpenAI will test a merged Chat and Work experience in ChatGPT with a small group. Separately, from December, Plus, Pro and Business plans draw ChatGPT and Codex use from one allowance measured in tokens. The email and OpenAI's help page weren't fetched.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The merge test:** users can *"bring in files and plugins and ask ChatGPT to build a website, create a presentation or do things on the web while they review and guide its work."* Codex stays a separate app on desktop.
- **One allowance:** *"Starting in December, Plus, Pro and Business plans will draw ChatGPT and Codex use from one token-measured allowance, but Instant chat won't count."*
- **No numbers yet:** *"OpenAI's published guidance gives no plan-by-plan token amounts."*
- **Context:** two days earlier OpenAI doubled Pro and Business Premium limits ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-10-29]]). Anthropic moved the other way on the same week, removing Ultra's five-hour window ([[anthropic-max-ultra-plan-rename-2026-10-30]]). Both labs are reworking how coding-agent use is metered.
