## Summary

On 2026-10-30 Anthropic renamed its Max 5x plan "Max" ($100 a month) and Max 20x "Ultra" ($200 a month), and began removing the five-hour usage limit for verified Ultra accounts. Weekly limits don't change. This page uses Digg's summary and AINews's recap (latent.space, 10-29/30), which cites Anthropic's posts. Anthropic's announcement itself wasn't fetched.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Names and prices:** *"Max 5x is now "Max" at $100/month, and Max 20x is now "Ultra" at $200/month."* (AINews)
- **The limit:** *"Verified Ultra accounts no longer have the 5-hour session limit."* It starts with most existing subscribers. Newer accounts keep a temporary limit until verified, which Anthropic calls an anti-fraud measure.
- **No more usage overall:** *"The weekly allowance does not increase."* (Digg)
- **Why it matters for Claude Code users:** the five-hour window could stop a long debugging session or refactor while weekly usage remained.
- **Unverified economics (AINews relaying @teortaxesTex citing SemiAnalysis):** *"Anthropic's API margin is put at about 92%"*; Opus on subscriptions breaks even *"only below about 21.3% utilization"*; subscriptions bring in *"about 18% of revenue while using more than 50% of compute"*. A reconstruction, not Anthropic's figures; the S-1 is the place to check them.
