## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The creating gap**: MIT studied 300 enterprise AI deployments; **95% produced no measurable P&L impact** — *"the models were fine, the deployments died"* (couldn't talk to legacy DBs, pass compliance, or survive handoff to ops). Plus 42% abandon most AI initiatives (S&P, up from 17%), 40%+ agentic projects cancelled by 2027 (Gartner), 88% use AI (McKinsey). *"Near-universal adoption, near-universal failure to convert. Your salary lives in that gap."*
- **Definition**: *"closes the last mile between an AI product and real enterprise value"*; Palantir — *"FDE responsibilities look similar to a startup CTO."* **Not** consultant / solutions-architect / rebrand.
- **Three hats**: software engineer (production code on their infra) + business consultant (map processes, translate pain→scope) + product manager (feed pain back to core product). = *"a founding CTO embedded in someone else's company, with your company's engineering resources behind you."*
- **Market**: postings 643 (Apr 2025) → 5,330 (Apr 2026) = **729%**; **$11.5B raised in one week (Jun 2026)** — Anthropic $1.5B JV (Blackstone/Goldman/H&F) + OpenAI "The Deployment Company" $10B w/ TPG to hire thousands.
- **Comp ladder**: ~$160K entry / ~$286K mid (Palantir median ~$215K) / $300K base+equity senior / ~$610K staff / $1.0–1.2M principal at frontier labs; **senior FDE @ Anthropic/OpenAI $785K+**. No PhD/papers/algorithm-puzzles needed.
- **Three employer tiers**: Tier 1 frontier labs (most selective, rarely entry) / Tier 2 applied-AI startups (~half comp, best growth path) / Tier 3 Fortune 500 ($150–250K, most postings least leverage). *"Same work, priced completely differently."*
