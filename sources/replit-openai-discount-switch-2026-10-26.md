## Summary

The Next Web (Cristian Dina, 2026-10-26) on a paywalled report in The Information: Replit moved most of its AI spend from Anthropic to OpenAI after OpenAI offered more than 30% off, and Rippling and DataCamp report similar shifts. TNW says it hasn't checked the figures itself. **This is a price war at the customer level,** the first the wiki holds with named customers, against the S-1's 900% net revenue retention figure the same day.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Replit:** moved most spend *"after OpenAI offered a discount of more than 30%"*; the contract is *"worth over $100M a year"*. Anthropic *"would not extend that discount without a bigger spending commitment"*.
- **Replit's CEO, Amjad Masad:** *"It was a decision based on both cost, partnership, and performance."* And: *"Replit remains model agnostic."*
- **Rippling:** OpenAI now handles *"52% of the HR software company’s coding work, up from 16% in May"*; Anthropic's share *"fell from 57% to 18%"*.
- **DataCamp:** OpenAI's pricing *"cut his costs by 80% to 90%"* (the CEO, to The Information).
- **Seat deals:** a US bank's ChatGPT seats were doubled *"at no extra charge"*; it pays *"under $7 per user"*.
- **Caveat:** *"TNW has not independently verified the figures."* All numbers trace to The Information.
