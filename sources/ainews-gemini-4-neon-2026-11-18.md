## Summary

Latent Space's 2026-11-18 roundup. Google DeepMind shipped Gemini 4 Neon, a mid tier between Hydrogen and Carbon, its fourth Gemini 4 model in about seven weeks, and retired Argon. The figures below are third-party numbers as relayed by AINews; the underlying leaderboards were not opened.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Line-up:** *"the 1.5 month old Argon is now retired"*. Neon is faster and cheaper than Carbon but *"technically outperforms Carbon by some measures despite being a smaller model"*.
- **Price and specs:** *"Neon costs $3 for input, $0.15 for cached input and $15 for output per million tokens. It has a 1M-token context window and four effort levels."*
- **Rollout:** paid Gemini API, AI Studio, Android Studio, Gemini for business, and Antigravity for Pro and Ultra subscribers; in the Gemini app as "Deeper analysis".
- **Cadence** (Arena): *"gaps of 28 days between Argon and Carbon, 15 between Carbon and Hydrogen, and six between Hydrogen and Neon."*
- **Artificial Analysis:** *"At high effort, Neon matched Carbon’s 60 on the Intelligence Index for $3.74 per task."* (The brief's "61" headline is the article's subtitle, *"scores 61 on the Intelligence Index"*, presumably at xhigh; the body doesn't say.)
- **Vals:** *"Neon placed second on the Vals Index at 70.9%, about 0.4 points behind Fable 5.2 at less than half the cost per task."*
- **CursorBench 4.1:** *"Neon scored 63.7% at default effort, up from Hydrogen’s 57.3%."*
- **Agent Arena:** debuted second *"with 1,336 points across about 4,200 sessions"*.
- **Also in the issue:** Anthropic's $83 IPO pricing (see [[anthropic-ipo-priced-nasdaq-debut-2026-11-19]]); Palo Alto Networks agreed to buy Applied Compute *"in a $4.5B all-stock deal"*.
