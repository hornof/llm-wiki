## Summary

TechCrunch (2026-10-30), citing the Financial Times: Blackstone, the anchor investor in the $63B financing for Anthropic's chips, told investors it now expects Anthropic to get a high junk rating after the IPO. It had earlier predicted investment grade before 2027. It's a forecast by one investor, not a rating.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The forecast:** Blackstone now *"expects double B range rating, the top tier of speculative “junk” grade"*.
- **The reversal:** Blackstone *"had said the opposite"*: investment grade *"before 2027 rolls around"*.
- **Link to the lease deal:** Blackstone is *"the anchor investor in a recent $63 billion deal to finance Anthropic’s chips"* ([[anthropic-broadcom-63b-lease-financing-2026-10-27]]).
- **No comment:** Anthropic and Blackstone declined.
