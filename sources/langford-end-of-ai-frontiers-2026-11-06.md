## Summary

John Langford's blog post (hunch.net, 2026-11-06) announcing that AI Frontiers, the research sub-organization he helped found inside Microsoft Research in 2023, is over. AIF was reorganized into Microsoft AI in early September. Langford says MAI's ex-Inflection leadership runs a scaling-only philosophy that had no room for research, and that he and the rest of AIF's founding leadership have left.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **What AIF was.** Formed after ChatGPT to organize research more tightly than MSR usually did. Size: *"the small size of AIF (~50 people)"*. Output included the Autogen orchestration framework and the Fara computer-use agent; the NYC group worked on the Next Latent objective, Dion3 and the Full Bandwidth Transformer.
- **The reorg.** *"In early September we were reorged into Microsoft AI"*. Langford: *"The core philosophy there is scaling, and aggressively not research."* He adds that *"there was plenty of evidence that they just did not really want us"*.
- **The case for research, with a number.** *"the Bytedance experiments suggest a variant of the Full Bandwidth Transformer might be comparable to a model of twice the size"*. (Langford's characterization of third-party results; not checked here.)
- **Departures.** *"Every other founding AIF leadership team member seems to have come to a similar conclusion."* He declined to return to MSR because research there has *"no path to real use"*.
- **Consequence.** No internships with the AIF group in the coming year.
- **Context the wiki already holds:** Microsoft retreated from the consumer chatbot race into enterprise Copilot on 2026-09-25 ([[microsoft]]). This post describes the research side of the same period.
