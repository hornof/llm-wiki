## Summary

Techmeme, citing Bloomberg, reports that the "Customer B" in a SpaceX filing is Anthropic, and that this customer made up 34.5% of SpaceX's Q3 revenue, more than the US government. The filing itself and Bloomberg weren't fetched; this page uses Digg's summary. Reported 2026-10-28.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Share of revenue:** the filing lists the customer *"as accounting for 34.5% of SpaceX's Q3 revenue"*, which makes it the largest single customer, ahead of the US government.
- **The identification is Bloomberg's, not the filing's.** The filing says "Customer B".
- **The monthly figure is social, not reported:** an X post says *"paying about $3 billion a month"*. That fits the S-1's own figure of about $3.1B a month once all capacity is delivered ([[anthropic-s1-public-filing-2026-10-26]]), but nobody in the reporting states it.
