## Summary

Simon Willison, 2026-11-02, updates a chart of AI adoption from the Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS). Apollo made the original in September 2025, and it seemed to show adoption falling. Willison redid it with GPT-6 Astra. Large-company adoption rose, but the question changed wording in November 2025, so the before and after numbers aren't comparable. From the 2026-10-19 collection the survey says "Super Intelligence" instead of "AI", because of Executive Order 14434.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The survey:** every two weeks it *"goes to 200,000 businesses out of 1.2 million total"*; each business answers every twelve weeks.
- **Large companies (250+ employees):** in September 2025 they said yes *"around 12% of the time"* to using AI *"in producing goods or services"*. Under the new *"any business function"* wording they said yes *"around 30% of the time"* by February 2026, *"and by October 2026 that had increased to around 40%."*
- **Not comparable:** *"the answers to the question before and after November 17th are not directly comparable."* The jump from 12% to 30% is mostly the wording.
- **The rename:** *"Beginning with the October 19, 2026 collection, instrument wording was updated due to Executive Order 14434."* The question now reads *"did this business use Super Intelligence or SI (formerly referred to as Artificial Intelligence or AI)"*.
- **Willison's open question:** whether the new word changes the answers. The first results under it were due the Thursday after the post.
