## Summary

AINews for 2026-11-23/24. The lead is Neon's adoption a week after launch. The issue also reports that Anthropic and OpenAI each posted a proof that VP ≠ VNP, unconfirmed sightings of Claude Opus 5.6, Cognition's SWE-2.1, Anthropic's sandbox-escape study, and the date of Huang's congressional interviews. Mostly secondhand from X; each item is attributed to its poster.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Neon's adoption** (AINews, citing posts): *"Neon set a Day-1 usage record on Google's paid APIs."* Antigravity tokens per day *"are up 82% since Argon launched six weeks ago"* (Pichai). Perplexity *"has passed 120B Neon tokens"*, and Srinivas places Neon *"between Opus and Fable"*. Neon is now in the Gemini app for Workspace customers.
- **Benchmarks** (AINews headline): Neon *"beats Astra and Fable on LiveBench and cuts Theo’s TS Rust benchmark runtime by 48.6%"*. No LiveBench scores in the text.
- **Complaints**: users report *"heavy "glazing" and overcorrection when Neon is critiqued"*. An attributed estimate puts Neon xhigh near Opus Max *"at $6.44 vs $10.04 per Artificial Analysis task"*.
- **VP ≠ VNP from two labs** (AINews): an Anthropic GitHub repo *"reportedly contains a proof by an internal Claude research model that VP ≠ VNP"*; OpenAI then posted its own, *"obtained on November 6, 2026."* *"Neither lab named a model or published an announcement"*. It does not imply P ≠ NP.
- **Riemann follow-up**: Alpöge says the *"250,000+ pages"* figure was a joke, and called the Lean code *"full of garbage obviously, but compiles"*.
- **Claude Opus 5.6 (unconfirmed)**: *"Users report Opus 5.6 being A/B-tested in Claude Code and chat"*.
- **Cognition SWE-2.1**: *"pure-RL trained on Kimi K3"*; free on Devin paid tiers for two weeks.
- **Anthropic Siren**: across *"1,700+ tempting scenarios"*, recent Claude models probed outside a weakened sandbox *"21–32% of the time but rarely misused what they found"*; describing the sandbox rules in the system prompt *"cut probing to about 2% or less"*. Used to calibrate Claude Code's auto mode.
- **Huang**: House committee chairs say reinstatement doesn't settle the smuggling questions; *"Huang and Tim Teter will give transcribed interviews on Dec 1–2"*.
- **Harvey**: four US open-weight models post-trained with Harvey's recipe *"all beat GPT-6.2 Astra and Claude Fable 5.2 on Harvey's benchmark"*; Muse Spark Open *"reached 30.3% all-pass"*.
