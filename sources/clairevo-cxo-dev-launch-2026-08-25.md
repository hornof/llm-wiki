## Summary

[[claire-vo|Claire Vo]] — creator of ChatPRD (the wiki's *"$1M, one-employee"* solo-founder exemplar), host of the "How I AI" podcast (100+ episodes), ex-CPO/CTO at LaunchDarkly — announces **CXO.dev**, an **AI-transformation consulting firm** for *"AI-pilled leaders,"* explicitly *"by builders, not consultants."* Notably she **gives up the solo-founder hat** to co-found it with **Zach Davis** (2 decades IC + eng leadership at Atlassian and LaunchDarkly). Folds into [[ai-native-organizations]] (the builder-led AI-transformation-consulting category, alongside [[alex-lieberman|Lieberman's]] @businessbarista practice) and is the launch anchor for the new [[claire-vo]] page. *(Launch-day promotional thread; self-asserted.)*

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The category: AI-transformation consulting "by builders, not consultants"** — *"AI operating models for scaled software companies."* A builder-led counter-positioning to traditional (McKinsey/Deloitte-style) consulting; the same *AI-transformation demand* [[businessbarista-enterprise-ai-asks-2026-08-25|Lieberman's 10 asks]] catalog, now with a named builder-led firm attacking it.
- **"Giving people Claude or ChatGPT and calling it a day" is not transformation** — the load-bearing insight: tool access ≠ adoption. Real team-level change is three-part: **technical readiness + an agent-ready operating model + (the hard one) cultural change.** *"There was so much more… we had to bring along non-believers, and people were stepping on each other's toes (and tbh, we weren't going that much faster)."*
- **Three service lines**: (1) build your team's AI-transformation roadmap; (2) work with eng/product/design to change how you ship — *incl. getting your repo agent-ready* (the [[garry-tan|Tan]] *"systems of record / codebase must become agent-ready"* claim as a service offering); (3) coach/train teams on tools they already have.
- **Origin story**: paired at LaunchDarkly as *"the AI-pilled exec and the nearly-10-year-veteran engineering leader who thought I was crazy"* — *"the skeptic and the chaos agent."* The 100+ "How I AI" episodes surfaced the recurring behind-the-scenes question *"Are people really moving this fast?"* — demand pull into the consulting business.
- **Early customers**: Amplitude, Chime, GCAI. Launching a **network of AI-pilled ex-operators and engineers** to staff engagements.
- **Narrative reversal**: the solo-founder poster child ([[ai-native-organizations|ChatPRD one-person-$1M]]) *"gives up the solo hat"* for a co-founded team + operator network — a live counter-datapoint to the "solo founder is the future" thesis.
