## Summary

TechCrunch (2026-10-22), relaying CNBC's unnamed-source report: [[openai]] is close to raising up to $30B at a $1.25T pre-money valuation, led by Coatue with UAE investors. The deal is still in talks and OpenAI declined to comment. The valuation is **lower** than the $1.4T Bloomberg reported for the same $30B about a month earlier.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Reported, not confirmed:** up to $30B; $1.25T pre-money; Coatue leading; UAE investors, possibly paired with a UAE compute deal for OpenAI.
- **Valuation path:** $852B post-money in March 2026 (the last closed round). The wiki recorded "early talks at $1.2T–$1.5T" on 2026-09-17. Bloomberg reported $1.4T in late September. CNBC now reports $1.25T.
- **IPO:** OpenAI filed confidential IPO paperwork in June 2026. No listing date.
- **Anthropic context in the same piece:** Anthropic raised $65B at $965B in May 2026. CNBC reports Anthropic is seeking $1.5–2T at IPO. TechCrunch says a public S-1 is rumoured. *(The wiki already has the 2026-09-28 prospectus filing and the 10-20 S-1 leak on [[anthropic]].
