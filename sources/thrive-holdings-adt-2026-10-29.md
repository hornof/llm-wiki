## Summary

Thrive Holdings, the holding-company spinout of Josh Kushner's Thrive Capital, bought the home-security company ADT. OpenAI is Thrive Holdings' partner and an investor, and supplies models and forward-deployed engineers to its companies. No AI products were announced.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Price:** *"$10 per share or about $7.6 billion in equity"*; *"the total enterprise value of the deal is $15.3 billion"*.
- **Model:** hold companies long-term and revitalize them with AI. Thrive *"holds over 75 companies"*.
- **OpenAI's role:** it *"supplies AI models and tech as well as people, otherwise known as forward-deployed engineers"*.
- **Plans:** *"ADT will form the foundation of Thrive Holdings’ smart home security business"*. No products named.
