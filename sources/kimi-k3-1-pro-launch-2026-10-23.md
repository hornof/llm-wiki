## Summary

A Digg aggregation (2026-10-23) of Moonshot's launch post, the Kimi API docs, SiliconANGLE and Arena. [[moonshot-ai|Moonshot]] shipped **Kimi K3.1 Pro**, a multimodal MoE with a 1M-token context, alongside a Highspeed variant it says is 2–3× faster. Both are live on Kimi.ai, Kimi Code and the API. **Pro's open weights are planned by 2026-11-04** and weren't downloadable at launch. No license is stated.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Size:** 2.8T total and **104B active**, per the page. [[kimi-k3]] was 2.8T total with ~50B active, so the active count roughly doubled if both are right. *(Not checked against a model card.)*
- **Benchmarks:**
  - Arena Code Arena WebDev: #8 at 1,692, against K3 Max at #17 with 1,655. That's one category, not an overall rank.
  - Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index: 55, against 44 for K3 and 58 for Opus 5.5 (via a social post).
  - Per SiliconANGLE, Pro's cyber benchmark scores trail GPT-6 Astra and Opus 5.5. **Moonshot says it removed cybersecurity data from post-training** for safety.
  - Unverified social posts claim it beats Opus 5.5 and Astra on most of Kimi's own benchmarks.
- **Price:** $2/$10 per M tokens, per a social post, against $4/$20 for Opus 5.5. Not checked against the API price list.
- **Caveat:** VentureBeat and RuntimeWire report allegations of undisclosed paid creator promotion.
