## Summary

Digg summary (2026-10-19) of a Bloomberg report, relayed by Reuters. Nscale is **in talks** to sell about $45 billion of compute to [[anthropic|Anthropic]] from a second building at its Monarch campus in West Virginia: 460 MW on NVIDIA Vera Rubin chips. The report says this is **separate from the August deal of about the same size, which covers the first Monarch building.** Anthropic reportedly also has a right of first refusal on an expected third building.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Status:** talks, not a signed deal. Reuters says details could change and that it could not verify the report. Neither company commented.
- **Capacity:** 460 MW, Vera Rubin, second Monarch building, West Virginia.
- **The August deal:** Bloomberg cites Nscale's IPO filing, which puts existing Anthropic agreements at up to about $44.6 billion. That is the first figure the wiki holds for the 2026-08-26 deal from a filing rather than press coverage, and it places that deal in the first Monarch building.
- **If both close,** Anthropic's commitments to this one supplier would be roughly $90 billion, with an option on a third building.
- **Not used:** the brief's draft says *"$98M per megawatt… half what hyperscalers pay for on-demand cloud compute."* The per-MW division is arithmetic; the hyperscaler comparison has no source and compares a multi-year capacity deal with on-demand pricing.
