## Summary

OpenAI says it has agreements with four outside groups to assess model alignment and safeguards from inside the lab. OpenAI's own post ([Our first phase of embedded assessments](https://openai.com/index/our-first-phase-of-embedded-assessments), dated 2026-11-13 per Digg) returned 403 and was not read. This page rests on Digg's write-up, which draws on OpenAI's post and Apollo Research's 2026-11-16 announcement. The announcements contain plans, not results.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Who:** Apollo Research, METR, Transluce and Goodfire. Access *"can include model checkpoints, code, training and evaluation data, monitoring systems and researcher discussions, depending on the engagement."*
- **Apollo:** six evaluators inside OpenAI for four months (*"six evaluators will work within OpenAI for four months"*), on whether training rewards attempts to undermine oversight and whether that reasoning would be detectable. Two public reports planned.
- **METR:** a *"limited alignment assessment"* of OpenAI's largest reinforcement-learning run. A broader monitor assessment is preliminary.
- **Transluce:** reviews OpenAI's post-training alignment evaluations, with a follow-on of about eight weeks (*"approximately eight weeks in early 2027"*) planned.
- **Goodfire:** researchers who study model internals, mainly serving OpenAI's own researchers.
- **What OpenAI can hold back** (Apollo's terms, via Digg): OpenAI may withhold information for technical, security or IP reasons, but must say what it withheld and why, and *"cannot withhold information or staff access simply because the information is unfavorable."* It cannot redact Apollo's verdicts, but it can redact supporting evidence: *"Readers may therefore get the conclusion without being able to inspect all the receipts."*
- **Working conditions:** OpenAI picks which employees Apollo may talk to; evaluators use OpenAI hardware; their channels can be monitored; no Slack access by default.
- **Payment:** Apollo and Goodfire are paid, not contingent on results; METR and Transluce take no assessment payment but may take API credits or compute.
- **Apollo's safeguard:** "meta-transparency", the right to say publicly what access it got, what was withheld and what pressure it felt, without OpenAI's prior review.
