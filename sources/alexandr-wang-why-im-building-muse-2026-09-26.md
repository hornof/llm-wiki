## Summary

A **manifesto for [[meta|Muse]]** from Alexandr Wang. It is the first primary statement of intent the wiki holds on a product it has now tracked from four angles — an [[amazon|Amazon block]], a rave NYT review, per-user persistent Linux VMs, and [[gregisenberg-escalate-to-human-button-2026-09-25|Isenberg's complaint]] that it cannot hand anything to a human.

**It contains no mechanism, no capability claim and no number.** Captured for the positioning, and because the positioning sits interestingly against what the product has actually run into.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

The argument, such as it is:

> *"Most people never say what they want. It's not because we don't want. We want fiercely and constantly… When we can't name our dreams, we mostly can't reach them. The world is a maze of forms and gatekeepers and schedules and obstacles, and most dreams die by a thousand paper cuts long before they are said aloud."*

> *"Every unrealized want is a quiet tragedy of human potential."*

The product framing:

> *"Imagine every person on earth with a **second mind** beyond their own. **A general manager whose whole job is to find out what you want and make sure it happens.** Someone that hears the half-sentence, 'I feel like there's something more I could be doing,' and hel[ps]…"*

And the closing bet:

> *"Here's the best part: **we have no idea what people will want.** The world until now has been shaped by the small number of fanatics who have somehow found a way to make their wants real. But we've never seen humanity with **every single person's agency fully switch[ed on]**."*

## Why it's worth keeping

**Not for what it says — for what it collides with.** The stated job is *"find out what you want and make sure it happens,"* and the wiki's record of what actually happens is:

- **Amazon blocked it from shopping** four days after it shipped with payment authority — a gatekeeper, which is precisely what the manifesto says the agent exists to get past.
- The **NYT raved** about it tracking credit-card spending — the mundane end of "make sure it happens."
- **[[greg-isenberg|Isenberg]]**: *"there's the 1 thing a week that needs an actual person. Right now I'm the person."* — i.e. the general manager cannot escalate.

**"A maze of forms and gatekeepers" is a good description of the problem, and Amazon is now one of the gatekeepers.** The manifesto treats obstacles as friction to be routed around; the first month suggests some of them are counterparties with their own interests.
