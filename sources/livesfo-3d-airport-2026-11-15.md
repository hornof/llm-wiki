## Summary

A web app that shows SFO airport in 3D with every aircraft live, built from ADS-B data and the SFO flight board. The clip is the site's help text; it doesn't say who built it or how, and it doesn't mention AI. Clipped 2026-11-15 as inspiration.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **What it shows:** *"every aircraft at its gate, taxiing, lining up, taking off and landing, from ADS-B and the SFO flight board."*
- **Views:** Explore, Follow (including a cockpit view), Tower (the SFO tower cab), TV (films landings, take-offs and pushbacks by itself) and Fly.
- **Terms:** free, no ads, no sign-up; donations cover servers and data feeds. Visits counted with a random browser id, no cookies.
