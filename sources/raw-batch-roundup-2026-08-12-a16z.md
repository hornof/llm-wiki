## Summary

An a16z clip (2026-08-12) of **[[garry-tan|Garry Tan]]** (YC CEO) on the a16z podcast ("Garry Tan on YC, First Principles Thinking, and Progress in the Age of AI"), naming a mega-trend: **the experienced 35–45-year-old AI-native founder**. - *"If you take that person, **suddenly there's 400 of those people. You can outperform an entire department of any Mag 7.**"*
