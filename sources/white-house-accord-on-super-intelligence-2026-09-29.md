## Summary

A one-page voluntary pledge signed at the White House on **2026-09-29** by President Trump and six AI leaders. It commits signatories to four layers of safety controls and audits, including an independent external auditor or evaluator. It was posted on Trump's Truth Social account; no whitehouse.gov text was found. The same day Trump signed an executive order directing agencies to say "super intelligence" instead of "artificial intelligence". A **Super Intelligence Force** followed on 2026-10-04. Fetched as a backlog item on 2026-10-21, because Anthropic's 10-15 evaluator post cited the Accord and the wiki had no record of it.

Sources: the Crowell & Moring client alert above (provisions, executive orders, task force); Washington Examiner (date and title, 2026-09-29); search-result summaries of Free Press Journal, Bernama and Deccan Chronicle (signatories); CFR and pebblous.ai commentary (criticism). The full text was not obtained. The Forbes and America First Policy reviews returned 403.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Signatories (as reported):** Sundar Pichai (Google), [[dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]] (Anthropic), Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), [[greg-brockman|Greg Brockman]] (OpenAI), [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]] (xAI) and [[jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] (NVIDIA), with Trump. **Microsoft and Amazon are not on it.** OpenAI signed through its president, not [[sam-altman|Altman]]. *(Names from secondary reports; Crowell and the Examiner name no one.)*
- **The four layers** (Crowell's paraphrase):
  1. Internal controls that monitor models in training and deployment for cyber, bio and chemical risk, and stop models hacking or gaining unintended access to systems.
  2. An internal team that checks those controls work and fixes problems.
  3. **An independent external auditor or evaluator** that assesses whether the controls work.
  4. **An independent board committee** that oversees the internal team and the auditors and receives their reports.
- **Quoted language:** *"every company is responsible for developing its own technology safely"*; signatories will *"meet regularly to establish standards"*; it may make sense *"over time"* to put these steps into law.
- **Not binding.** Trump called it *"morally binding."* Crowell calls it voluntary.
- **What critics note is missing:** no enforcement, no penalties, no timeline, no requirement to publish audit results, and no definition of an "independent" evaluator. **The company being audited picks its auditor.**
- **Issued with it:**
  - EO *"Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence"* (09-29): agencies use "super intelligence" in place of "artificial intelligence" in communications, and draft a legal definition of "Super Intelligence" within 60 days.
  - **Super Intelligence Force** (10-04, Truth Social), led by DNI Jay Clayton, **FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson**, Under Secretary of War Emil Michael and OPM Director Scott Kupor. It owes a report on AI risks and opportunities within 120 days.
