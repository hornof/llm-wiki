## Summary

A one-line question from [[sarah-guo|Sarah Guo]] (Conviction) on 2026-09-13: *"best multi agent harness outside of the labs?"* Small as a source, but it is a **demand-side datapoint on the [[domain-specific-harness]] thread** from the investor side, posted two days after the YC-batch observation and the same week [[pieter-levels|Levels]] argued the frontier labs will absorb the category.

The replies are the content: a working practitioner shortlist of what counts as a multi-agent harness today.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The question presupposes the category.** "Multi agent harness" is used with no definition and no hedging, by an investor, to a professional audience — and the replies answer in kind. That is the vocabulary-adoption signal, independent of any single answer.
- **The qualifier is the interesting part: *"outside of the labs."*** Guo takes as given that the labs have the best ones internally and is asking what exists beyond them. That is **exactly the asymmetry Levels named** — frontier teams holding unreleased models and unmetered compute — restated as a shopping constraint rather than an objection. See [[levelsio-harness-startups-software-is-dead-2026-09-13]].
- **Named in replies** (each mentioned once or twice, no consensus):
  - **AmpCode** — named by [[hamel-husain|Hamel Husain]] and by Ryan Carson; the only name appearing twice.
  - **[[cognition]]** — Husain, alongside AmpCode.
  - **Devin** — Carson (Cognition's agent, so arguably the same answer as above).
  - **openscout** (`openscout.app`) — offered by @arach with an explicit disclaimer: *"neither the best, nor a harness, but it's multi agent and outside of the labs so it meets some of the requirements."* Described as a native (non-Electron) app that *"plays well with Herdr and turns every harness into multi agent harnesses."*
  - **Herdr** — surfaced here as a composition layer under openscout, and previously as a session-management substrate in [[croovies-loop-orchestrator-mission-note-2026-09-10]].
- **Nobody named a domain-specific harness.** Every answer is a *general-purpose* multi-agent coding harness. For a thread about what exists "outside of the labs," in the same week a YC batch was reported to consist of domain-specific harnesses, that absence is worth noting — though the question asked about multi-agent harnesses specifically, which is a narrower thing.
