## Folded here

- **Australia's Digital Duty of Care Act** (Digg citing AP). Parliament passed it *"with maximum fines of 5% of a tech company’s global revenue"*; services must *"proactively reduce the risk of harm"*. The US ambassador and the opposition warn about censorship of lawful speech. The brief says AI chatbots are named explicitly; the Digg text doesn't say so, and AP was paywalled.
- **New York's five-year AI chatbot toy ban** (Digg citing Governor Hochul). Signed November 24; it starts *"90 days after the November 24 signing"*, so it misses this holiday season. Covers manufacturing, sale and distribution, with *"potential civil penalties of up to $15,000 a day"*. A poster quoted on Digg dates the start to February 22, 2027, and says it covers toys for children 12 and younger.
- **Amodei answers Axios** (Digg citing Axios, TechCrunch, Forbes). Axios reported that some Anthropic employees support handing authority to superintelligent AI. *"Amodei says humans should make political decisions"*, separating governance by AI from task automation. TechCrunch's headline has him calling the accusation "absurd". Separately, AINews relays Axios reporting that many Anthropic and OpenAI staff think some unreleased models are too powerful to release ([[ainews-gemini-4-neon-frontier-return-2026-11-24]]).
- **Nasdaq 100 on track for a bear market** (Digg citing Bloomberg, Reuters, CNBC, FT). *"The Nasdaq 100 fell 4.7% in early trading on November 25, 2026, reaching 20% below its October record"*, an intraday reading. *"Nvidia dropped more than 8% after the open"*. Anthropic *"dropped roughly 9% to just under $75 on Wednesday"*, per the FT, after slipping below $83 on Tuesday. SoftBank *"fell 14.83%"*, SK Hynix *"lost 11.05%"*. A Futurum analyst: *"There are still concerns on the sustainability of AI demand, ability of the sector to finance the build-out, and questions around power supply"*.
- **SpaceX's Cochise County campus** (Digg citing SpaceX). *"a roughly $50 billion AI campus in Cochise County, Arizona, with $3.6 billion in promised grid upgrades"*; nine buildings, *"up to six gigawatts of power"*, battery storage *"of up to 24 gigawatt-hours"*. Musk calls it *"SpaceX Colossus 3"*. Each building has *"approximately 10 megawatts of natural-gas-fired standby generation"*. Arizona's attorney general asked county officials to delay the permit vote (Digg link title).
- **Microsoft's "Code Red"** (Digg citing Reuters, The Verge). After the November 12–13 Microsoft 365 outage, which lasted *"roughly 24 hours"*, responders exchanged *"more than 11,000 messages across 11 Teams chats"*. Goal: recovery worldwide *"within an hour"*. *"Microsoft also plans to deploy AI agents to help engineers diagnose problems more quickly"*. Microsoft said the cause was a Windows scalability defect, not AI agents.
- **B300 servers offered in Shenzhen** (Digg citing the Washington Post). Vendors *"quoted $3 million to $3.4 million for servers purportedly containing eight Nvidia Blackwell B300 chips"*, unauthenticated, against a US retail estimate of *"roughly $500,000 to $600,000"* (Kyle Chan, Brookings).
- **Harvey post-trains US open-weight models** (Digg citing Harvey). *"Muse Spark Open reached 30.3% all-pass on LAB, the first model to pass 30% on the benchmark"*; Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, Beam 1.0 and Inkling 2 Small *"each surpassed 20% all-pass"*. Experimental checkpoints, no customer data.
- **OpenAI: the matrix-multiplication exponent is 9/4** (Digg citing OpenAI's math repo). OpenAI claims *"the exponent of square matrix multiplication is exactly 9/4, or 2.25, over every field"*, adding a matching lower bound for formal arithmetic circuits to an October upper-bound claim. The README reports a Lean formalization for division-free circuits. Digg: *"the available material does not establish independent validation"*.
- **Scott Aaronson** (quoted by Willison, https://simonwillison.net/2026/Nov/25/scott-aaronson/): *"It’s now obvious that we’ll be getting, not one-off solutions to this or that complexity question, but a whole new continent of mathematical truth"*.
- **Denny's restores Nvidia's founding-booth plaque** (Digg citing Casey Newton). Trivia; no page.
