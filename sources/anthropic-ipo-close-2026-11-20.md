## Summary

Anthropic's announcement (2026-11-20) that its IPO closed, with the underwriters buying all 147M optional extra shares. Folded in with it: AINews's IPO-day issue (latent.space, 2026-11-19, saved as `_raw/ainews-anthropic-ipo-largest-2026-11-19.txt`) and two Digg write-ups of the second trading day and the week's Nasdaq slide (`_raw/anthropic-ipo-day-two-digg-2026-11-20.txt`). The primary settles the deal size, which earlier pages gave before the option was exercised.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Final size** (Anthropic): 1,127,000,000 shares at $83 (*"an aggregate of 1,127,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock at a public offering price of $83.00 per share"*), including the option on 147M more (*"an additional 147,000,000 shares"*). Gross proceeds about $93.5B (*"approximately $93.5 billion"*), of which about $91.6B goes to Anthropic (*"approximately $91.6 billion was received by Anthropic"*). Anthropic sold 1,103,351,354 shares and selling stockholders 23,648,646 (*"The offering consisted of 1,103,351,354 shares sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 shares sold by certain selling stockholders"*).
- **The private placement, named** (Anthropic): 313,253,007 Class N shares at $83 (*"an aggregate of 313,253,007 shares of Class N common stock"*), about $26.0B (*"approximately $26.0 billion"*), to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, NVIDIA and TSMC Partners. IPO plus placement: about $119.5B gross (*"approximately $119.5 billion"*).
- **Dates** (Anthropic): registration effective 2026-11-18; trading on Nasdaq Global Select as ANT from 2026-11-19. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley led.
- **Earlier figures were the base deal.** The $81.34B on [[anthropic-ipo-priced-nasdaq-debut-2026-11-19]] and AINews's *"The $81.3B raised beats SpaceX's $75B (June) as the largest IPO ever"* are before the option. 980M + 147M = 1,127M, which matches.
- **First day** (AINews): opened $111.50, *"$28.50 (34.3%) above the $83 IPO price"*. **Closed at $97.17** (Digg: *"Thursday’s session had ended at $97.17, approximately 17% above the offering price"*).
- **Second day** (Digg citing WSJ, BBC, Fortune): down 8.5% (*"closed its second trading day down 8.5%"*), low of $83.05, five cents over the offer (*"trading as low as $83.05 before recovering"*). AP's corrected close: $88.91 (*"put the stock’s Friday loss at 8.5%, to $88.91"*). The Information's editor-at-large called demand *"fragile"* and said the drop could make future AI IPOs harder.
- **The market around it** (Digg citing FT and Bloomberg): Nasdaq 100 down 10.4% on the week (*"capping a 10.4% weekly slide"*), its worst since March 2020, and intraday in bear territory (*"the FT measured a 20% decline from its Oct. 6 intraday high"*). Nvidia fell about 14% on Friday (*"Nvidia lost about 14%"*).
- **From the S-1, via AINews** (secondary, not checked against the filing): Google owns 13.7% (*"Google owns 13.7% of Anthropic stock"*); Anthropic raised about $140B and holds about $132B (*"Anthropic raised ~$140B and still holds ~$132B on the balance sheet"*), which Tanay Jaipuria read as burning *"like under $10 billion"* to date. AMD agreed to invest $2.5B alongside the IPO (*"AMD agreed to invest $2.5B in Anthropic alongside the IPO"*).
- **Amodei's IPO remarks** (AINews quoting Anthropic): *"We will pace the training and deployment of AI systems, taking as long as is necessary to have confidence that they are safe and will remain under human control. We will not bend to either business or political pressure."*
- **Bear case** (AINews relaying FT Unhedged): a 20% return on about $1T of compute commitments needs about $602B a year of revenue (*"requires ~$602B/yr revenue"*), more than Alphabet or Apple earns.
