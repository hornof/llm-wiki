## Summary

Payments Dive (2026-08-19) on Stripe's announcement that it is acquiring OpenRouter, read alongside the price reporting collected by AIToolsReview (last updated 2026-08-25). **Stripe confirmed the deal; neither company disclosed a price.** Fetched on 2026-10-21 because the wiki recorded "Stripe acquires OpenRouter for $7B" from a Latent Space summary, and that figure anchors an argument on [[ai-margin-collapse]].

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Timeline:** Bloomberg reported a finalized agreement on 2026-08-16. Stripe announced on 2026-08-19 with a statement from Patrick Collison: *"Stripe is building the economic infrastructure for AI."* A Stripe spokesperson declined to comment on terms.
- **Price, as reported (none official):**
  - Bloomberg: over $7B.
  - New York Times: $7.5B, including about $1.5B to OpenRouter's founders.
  - Axios: above $8B in cash and stock.
  - Talks reportedly began near $10B in July (WSJ).
- **Close:** *"subject to customary closing conditions,"* expected *"within a matter of weeks"* (per AIToolsReview's summary of the announcement). **No confirmed close found as of 2026-10-21.** One outlet says "completed" but gives no closing date.
- **OpenRouter metrics (Payments Dive):**
  - More than 10 trillion tokens a day from about 10 million developers and companies.
  - About 400 models.
  - A 5.5% fee on credit purchases.
  - At least 10× yearly growth in LLM volume since its 2023 founding.
  - $153M raised, including $113M in May 2026.
- OpenRouter keeps its own brand and product. It's Stripe's largest acquisition, above Bridge ($1.1B).
