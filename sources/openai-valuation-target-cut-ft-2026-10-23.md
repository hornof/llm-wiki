## Summary

A Digg aggregation (2026-10-23) of the Financial Times, with CNBC, Bloomberg and Reuters. It explains the drop recorded on [[openai]] on 2026-10-22: **OpenAI cut its target from $1.4T to $1.25T pre-money because some investors resisted the higher price.** Morgan Stanley and Dragoneer had floated $1.4T to private-wealth clients, and demand was weaker than in past rounds. One person told the FT: *"There has been zero clamouring."*

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Why:**
  - Investor fatigue.
  - Some investors want to see how [[anthropic]] prices its IPO before committing to OpenAI.
- **Terms:**
  - Up to $30B, final size undecided, and possibly less.
  - Coatue and UAE investors in discussions to lead.
  - **Commitments due 2026-10-30** (FT).
- **IPO:** a person close to OpenAI told the FT the first half of 2027 is likely. Sarah Friar reportedly told staff in August it "will be a public company in 2027," while Altman said a month later that now would be "an ill-advised moment to go public."
