## Summary

TechCrunch (Marina Temkin, 2026-11-06): Crusoe confirmed it sold its unfinished southeastern Wyoming data center project to Google. Google had declined to be the project's customer and reportedly raised concerns about cost and schedule under Crusoe's management. Google now plans to build the campus itself.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The deal.** Price undisclosed, but Google *"announced plans to invest $17 billion to develop the data center campus"*. The project was planned to scale *"to 10 gigawatts of power capacity"*.
- **Crusoe.** Valued *"at $30.9 billion"*; raised *"a $3.9 billion round in September"* despite the setback. Still building for Google near Amarillo, Texas.
- **Crusoe's other customers.** Abilene, Texas for Oracle and OpenAI; *"a separate 900-megawatt campus next door for Microsoft"*; Meta; and a reported Jane Street deal of *"$13 billion"* over five years (Bloomberg).
- **TechCrunch's read.** Independent developers struggle to deliver at this scale given equipment, power and local opposition. That is TechCrunch's framing, not a claim from either company.
