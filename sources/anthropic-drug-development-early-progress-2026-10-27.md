## Summary

Anthropic's life sciences team (2026-10-27) reports on six drug-development partnerships (BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines, The Column Group) and on drug programs of its own. Each partner case covers a different stage, from molecule design to manufacturing paperwork to clinical-trial planning. **The post says the work is mostly preclinical.** All results are reported by Anthropic and its partners. The technical reports were not read for this page.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Generate (CAR T-cell design):** six scientists used Claude Code to propose designs for part of the therapy's genetic instructions. *"One design was about 70% shorter than the standard version"*, and in lab tests it let immune cells make *"roughly 10 times as many receptors"*. The brief's draft says the design was cut *to* 30% of standard length. The post says it was 70% shorter, which is the same thing.
- **Divergence Labs (food allergy antibodies):** Claude generated *"roughly 35,000 antibody designs in about a month"*. Early tests found three binders and 20 designs that dislodge IgE. *"Three of those 20 appeared to dislodge IgE more than the standard medication"*.
- **BigHat (lead optimization):** the lab made and tested *"about 1,010 variants in about 10 weeks"*. Partway through, Claude and an Anthropic researcher argued the standard stability metrics were misleading and proposed new ones. The best variants bound *"more than 10x more tightly"*.
- **Enveda (manufacturing records):** chemists had spent *"up to 75% of their time on paperwork"*. On 1,061 pages, *"Claude caught all 11 issues that human reviewers had spotted—and flagged seven they’d missed"*, and review time fell by 89% (*"reduced human review time by 89%"*). One regulatory draft *"took four hours, compared to about three months by hand"*.
- **The Column Group:** is setting up Pleiades, a company built to run more drug programs per person with Claude. Its first skill was *"evaluated on 45 target-disease pairs"*.
- **BMS:** Claude assesses clinical-trial plans for an early-stage drug. The quote is BMS's.
- **Anthropic now runs its own drug program.** It acquired Mithril Medicine, a one-person muscular dystrophy company. Its founder used Claude to coordinate *"11 external labs"* and went *"from an idea to promising results in mice in less than 18 months"*. Human trials could start *"as soon as late 2028"*.
- **Caveat in the post itself:** *"so far it is mostly preclinical"*.
