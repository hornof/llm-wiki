## Summary

Neha Mehra (Forbes, Aug 3 2026) makes the stats case that AI agents have inverted startup economics and made solo founding viable at scale — the mainstream-press version of the wiki's [[ai-native-organizations|tiny-team]] cluster, with numbers.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Solo-founded startups: 23.7% (2019) → 36.3% (mid-2025)** — the population-level receipt for [[naveengrao-100x-entrepreneurs-2026-08-19|Naveen Rao's "100x more entrepreneurs"]] claim.
- **Cost inversion:** a complete AI stack runs **$3K–$12K/year** vs **$80K–$120K/month** for the equivalent human team.
- **Capital gap:** 48% of angels funded solo founders in 2025 vs ~25% of VCs; solo founders kept a **median 75% more equity at exit**.
- **Named receipts:** Maor Shlomo / **Base44** — built alone, acquired by Wix for **$80M after 6 months**; Pieter Levels — $3M+/yr portfolio, zero employees; Ben Broca / Polsia — $1M ARR managing 1,100+ client companies solo.
- **[[dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]] prediction cited:** billion-dollar solo company by 2026 at **70–80% probability**, likely in dev tools or automated customer service.
- **Where the model fails (the useful caveat):** enterprise sales, regulated industries (healthcare, fintech), and scales where compute costs rival salaries.
