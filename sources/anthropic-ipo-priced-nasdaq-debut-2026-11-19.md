## Summary

Anthropic priced its IPO on 2026-11-18 at $83 a share, above the $70–78 range, and began trading on Nasdaq as ANT on 2026-11-19. It led the 2026-11-19 brief ([[dailybrief-roundup-2026-11-19]]). Digg's write-up of the pricing breaks down who gets the money; TechCrunch reports the opening trade and Dario Amodei's speech before the bell. Reuters' $2T story from the brief was not readable (JavaScript wall); TechCrunch carries the same $2T figure. Other sources: [Digg on the pricing](https://digg.com/tech/973rjks9), [Digg on the European retail slice](https://digg.com/tech/io68bprw).

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Price and size** (Digg): 980M shares at $83, $81.34B before fees (*"putting the base deal at $81.34 billion before fees"*). The earlier plan was 825M shares at $70–78.
- **Not all of it goes to Anthropic** (Digg): Anthropic sells 956,351,354 shares, about $79.38B gross (*"worth approximately $79.38 billion gross at the IPO price"*). Selling stockholders sell 23,648,646 shares, about $1.96B (*"worth approximately $1.96 billion gross"*), and Anthropic gets none of that.
- **Private placement** (Digg citing Axios): a $26B concurrent private placement (*"a $26 billion concurrent private placement"*) to investors including Nvidia, Google and Microsoft. Together with the IPO that is about $107.3B (*"roughly $107.3 billion"*). Underwriters have a 30-day option on up to 147M more shares (*"up to 147 million additional shares"*), not known to be exercised. Closing expected 2026-11-20.
- **Opening trade** (TechCrunch): $111.50 in the first minutes, 33% over the offer (*"shares were trading at $111.50, a 33% premium over the $83 IPO price"*). At that price the market cap is over $2T (*"would put the company’s market cap over $2 trillion"*); TechCrunch says only Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, Nvidia and Microsoft are worth more.
- **Cash** (TechCrunch): an expected $220B in reserves after the IPO, earlier rounds and the placements (*"an expected $220 billion in cash reserves"*). An analyst: *"Anthropic’s going to have more cash on hand than any company has ever had."*
- **Amodei before the bell** (TechCrunch): *"If we can show it’s possible to make the right choices on safety and still build an incredibly successful company, others in our industry will be inspired to do the same. We call this a race to the top."*
- **European retail** (Digg citing Bloomberg): $1.1B sold to retail investors in the UK, EU, Norway and Switzerland (*"Anthropic sold $1.1 billion worth of IPO stock to retail investors in the UK and continental Europe"*). Bloomberg calls the IPO *"the biggest initial public offering of all time."*
- **After the open** (Digg headline, not read in full): *"Anthropic shares fall 27% from post-IPO high, The Kobeissi Letter reports"*, with the shares *"less than 2% above their $83 IPO price."* One newsletter's report; no exchange data checked.
- **Also reported** (Digg sidebar, headlines only): *"Center for AI Safety says it joined coalition challenging Anthropic’s safety claims"*, and TechCrunch headlines on the seven founders cashing out nearly $2B and the IPO possibly delivering $156B to charity. None were read.
