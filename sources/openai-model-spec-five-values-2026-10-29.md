## Summary

OpenAI's 2026-10-29 update rebuilds its public Model Spec around five values and adds guidance for models that take consequential actions. openai.com returned 403, so this page uses Digg's summary of OpenAI's post, "An updated Model Spec for more capable and beneficial AI". The quotes are Digg's sentences, not OpenAI's.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The five values:** *"faithfulness to human intent, harm minimization, honesty and transparency, human agency, and care"*. Faithfulness to intent is called the foundation.
- **Why:** models taking more consequential actions *"need to understand human intentions and values, not just follow specific instructions"*.
- **What changed:**
  - An updated chain of command for resolving conflicting instructions.
  - A new subsection on how conversations, memories and other context should inform a model's read of a person's intent.
  - New defaults on supporting human agency and following social norms, and more guidance on side effects.
  - *"OpenAI retired much of the Spec’s detailed style guidance"*, saying style should adapt to the person.
  - *"The Spec now calls the model “GPT” rather than “the assistant.”"*
- **Not yet achieved:** *"OpenAI says its models do not yet fully reflect the Spec."* It's exploring new training methods and will report results later.
