## Summary

TechCrunch (2026-06-25) visits [[general-intuition|General Intuition]] as it announces a $320M round at a $2.3B valuation. The lab trains one agent model on gameplay clips from Medal, its sister company, using the recorded button presses as action labels, and runs the same model in games and on a quadruped robot. TechCrunch's follow-up of 2026-08-24 (https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/valor-point72-back-general-intuition-at-6b-valuation-as-ai-startup-pushes-into-robotics/) is recorded here too.

*(Rewritten 2026-11-15. The original page was written from the Daily Brief's headline without the article, read "$2.3B" as a total bet rather than the valuation, and was mostly `canonical-` filler. Both articles were fetched for this rewrite.)*

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The round:** *"General Intuition said it raised $320 million at a $2.3 billion valuation"*, bringing *"total disclosed funding to $454 million, after the $134 million round it raised at launch last October."* Led by Khosla Ventures.
- **The data advantage:** Medal's gameplay supplied *"the initial dataset"*, but *"the key ingredient wasn’t the gameplay footage; it was the action labels embedded in those clips"*.
- **One model, game to robot:** *"The same brain powering the agent playing the game is powering the robot"*, per de Witte. *"it took just eight minutes of real-world robotics data to fine-tune an AI model for the quadruped."*
- **Training setup:** its world model *"isn’t the product; it’s the training environment"*; the company wants to sell the agent model.
- **TechCrunch's caveat:** *"getting such a model to hold up in the physical world, at scale, hasn’t yet been done."*
- **Use of funds:** *"The vast majority of the round will go toward scaling compute capacity"*, through a CoreWeave deal.
- **Not for weapons:** *"No agents will be employed to harm humans."*
- **Follow-up, 2026-08-24:** General Intuition *"is in talks to raise funding at a $6 billion pre-money valuation"* from investors including Valor Equity Partners, Point72 Ventures and Seven Seven Six, per sources; *"General Intuition is still finalizing the round"*.
