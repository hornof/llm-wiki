## Summary

[[greg-isenberg|Greg Isenberg]] (2026-11-10) reacts in ten points to OpenAI's beta of **Businesses in ChatGPT**. Businesses can claim a profile, see engagement insights, and, if eligible, build a Business Agent that answers customer questions. OpenAI's announcement page (openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) returned 403 to a plain fetch and was not retried. The product description below comes from OpenAI's post as quoted in the clip.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The product (OpenAI's post):** *"Claim your business, manage your information, and get insights on how people engage with you."* Eligible businesses *"can also build a Business Agent to answer customers’ questions"*.
- **Isenberg's frame:** *"this could be the google my business moment for ai"*. In the 2010s, agencies made a living claiming and fixing local listings for businesses.
- **Ads can lead to the agent.** *"ads can now click through to a conversation with your agent instead of your website"*.
- **Product feed as SEO:** *"your product feed is your new seo"*.
- **What the dashboard hides.** It shows how often ChatGPT mentions you but *"HIDES the prompts and your competitors"*. And *"businesses can’t see the conversations, so nobody knows which chats turn into sales yet"*.
- **Scope today:** *"agents are only open to us ecommerce and online services right now"*. Isenberg expects local services next. That's his guess.
- **His own doubt:** *"i’m not sure people want to talk to a brand’s agent. i think they’ll want their own agent to talk to it for them"*.
- **Usage figure, from Isenberg rather than OpenAI here:** *"1.2 billion people use chatgpt every week"*. Not checked.
- **Replies:** fake reviews moving into ChatGPT, and a complaint that editing a profile requires *"a driver’s license or passport"*. A promoted tweet in the clip is ignored.
