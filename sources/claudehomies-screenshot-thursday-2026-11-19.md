## Summary

A weekly show-and-tell thread from 2026-11-19 where people post things they made with Claude. Most replies are a screenshot and a line; the clip keeps the text, not the images. Everything here is the poster's own description.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- [cartoart.net](https://cartoart.net/): turns any location into wall art. Top reply, 16 points.
- [tarotstudy.app](https://tarotstudy.app/): an online notebook for tarot study notes.
- A point-and-click adventure engine; *"Claude drew all the pixels, and wrote the puzzles."*
- [unbuiltbryan.com](https://unbuiltbryan.com/): interactive 3D models.
- A video game about the poster's childhood in Montana ([video](https://youtu.be/S2lXC4thbbM)). Custom engine, *"Every single line of code from scratch."* *"I started building it 10 days ago when fable 5.2 dropped."* Supports 10 players on a local host. Built *"with a single claude ultra plan and a little codex too."*
- [wogl.io](https://wogl.io/): learn shaders interactively and build landing pages with them.
- [agora-mesh](https://github.com/kn0sti/agora-mesh): a Rust tool that connects Claude Code on several machines, peer to peer, each with its own login. The author says *"it is not cheaper than one agent for small tasks"*.
- [playbotfight.com](https://playbotfight.com/): a pretend-money chart game. The poster's simulation of a 47%-win strategy risking 20% a trade: *"85 in 100 lose 90% of the account at some point, and about 3 in 100 end above where they started."*
- Also: a typing web game, a meal tracker, a mood-logging app in Swift, a plant-collection app, a stargazing centre's website, a first drawing study tutored by Claude.
