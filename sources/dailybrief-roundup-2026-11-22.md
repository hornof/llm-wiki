## Folded here

- **Dell, HPE, Lenovo push AI-server prices up to 45%** (Digg citing FT). Some buyers face *"price hikes reaching 45%"* months after signing, largely from memory costs, which JPMorgan says have risen *"more than 400% since the start of 2024"*. Trading firms are paying (*"We’ve never said no"*). XTX is suing Dell over a *"fixed server price of $549 million"*, saying Dell sought another $62–70M. Added to [[ai-margin-collapse]].
- **Nvidia falls 14% as Anthropic's IPO glow fades** (Digg citing Axios). *"Nvidia fell 14% on November 20 despite raising its 2027 revenue outlook"*; *"Nvidia had lost more than $2.5 trillion in market capitalization since its October 6 all-time high"*. Axios puts ANT's first-day high at $115.10 (*"first-day high of $115.10"*). Added to [[nvidia]].
- **Anthropic told some IPO banks to stick to four bankers** (Digg citing Reuters). Anthropic asked some of its 22 underwriters to *"limit teams to four senior bankers"* and sought six-month limits on work for OpenAI, Cursor, DeepSeek and Hugging Face. *"Bankers earned more than $500 million in fees"*. How many bankers signed the restrictions is unclear. Added to [[anthropic]].
