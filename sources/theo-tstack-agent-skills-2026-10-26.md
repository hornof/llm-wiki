## Summary

Theo Browne (t3.gg) published their daily agent skills as tstack, an MIT-licensed repo for Claude Code and Codex, announced on X on 2026-10-26 ([post](https://x.com/theo/status/2114623574371963360)). They had argued against publishing them before (*"I caved"*). Garry Tan quote-posted *"Publish your stack"* ([post](https://x.com/garrytan/status/2114739519622963671)). At fetch time the repo had 2.1k stars (*"Star 2.1k"*) and 128 forks (*"Fork 128"*).

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **What's in it:** skills triggered by a spoken phrase. Examples:
  - `full-send` takes a change all the way to a merged PR *"with nobody watching"*.
  - `full-stop` stops when the PR is ready for you to merge.
  - `council` *"Gets review from three models from different providers, and loops until they agree."*
  - `sol-loop` reviews with GPT Sol until both agents agree.
- **Cross-vendor review is the notable design choice:** two of the skills make models from different labs check each other's work.
- **The case for publishing, from Tan's replies:** one reply says *"publishing your skills is the new readme culture"*. Another says stacks are safe to share because *"the wiring is the hard part, and wiring does not transfer by reading."*
- **Owner's reply:** the wiki owner answered Tan's post with a link to their own published stack, `github.com/hornof/apb`.
