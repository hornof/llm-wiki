## Summary

jessy (**@goodhartproof**) posted from YC Demo Day on 2026-09-11: *"I was just at YC demo day yesterday. Besides hardware/physical things, everyone is just basically just building a domain-specific harness."* The reply thread turned into a compact articulation of the **harness-as-product** thesis — what the moat is when the model is a commodity, and a running skeptical counter ("is harness the new term for wrapper?"). Captured as the batch-level market signal that the [[loop-engineering|harness]] register has moved from practitioner vocabulary to **company shape**.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Headline observation**: at YC Demo Day, non-hardware startups converge on one shape — a **domain-specific harness**: the agent that runs one industry's work.
- **jessy on the unlock**: *"the key unlock is increasing the surface area of verifiable things."* — the verifier-first framing ([[loop-engineering]], [[samueljmcd-loop-engineering-verifier-bottleneck-2026-06-15|McDonald's verifier-discipline corrective]]) restated as the *market-expansion* variable, not just an engineering practice.
- **jessy on the next wedge** (twice, to two different repliers): *"the next wedge is domain RL, data and customer relationships."* — the harness itself is assumed to be copyable; the durable layer is domain RL + proprietary data + distribution.
- **Doubleright (@Doublerightvc)**: *"Feels like the model is becoming the commodity and the harness is the product. The real moat is probably the messy domain context, integrations, and distribution around it."* — the crispest single-line statement of the harness-as-product thesis in the thread; buyer-side mirror of [[ai-margin-collapse]].
- **Alexander Whitelaw (@AlexWhitelawCA)** — three post-frontier-model company shapes, all resting on the assumption that *"we should just be plugging frontier models into stuff and plugging new stuff into frontier models"*:
  1. **Harnesses** that plug models into domains — most advanced examples are GTM-automation companies, because GTM is qualitative and *"data governance is not really important. Nobody really sweats the small stuff as they do in finance/acct."*
  2. **Tooling-tools** (n8n, Zapier, Clay-for-GTM) — workflow automation where the domain is simple enough not to need category 1.
  3. **Dataset building** for domains like bio, *"after RLVR successes in math and coding,"* hoping for the same breakout. Whitelaw adds that *"there are lots of companies creating evaluation datasets to enable RLVR for drug development."*
- **Skeptical counter-current (unresolved in-thread)**: `@glass_hamlet` — *"Is harness the new term for wrapper then?"*; `@nareshrockyn` — *"I'm missing something, harness is not wrapper?"*; `@pareen` — *"how does one monetise a harness?"*; `@signalgaining` — *"Ah these harnesses are training the models..."*. None got an answer from jessy. **Recorded as the live objection**, not resolved.
- **Geographic corroboration**: Théo (@theositjar), Paris — *"This is 100% what people are doing right now even around us... Harness, tools, and agent stacks for specific usage."*
- **Hostile take** (@Huangs_law, quote-reposting @nautsimon_): mocks the harness discourse as derivative of a *"YC Paper Club"* talk — *"generating a harness around some painfully strict state machine."* Noted as the contrarian register; no substantive argument attached.
