## Summary

Digg's summary, 2026-11-02, of AP and FT reporting on the UK National Cyber Security Centre's annual report. Its head, Richard Horne, expects AI attacks on UK critical infrastructure by 2028. The NCSC thinks AI will mostly speed up attacks that already work, not find new flaws. The NCSC report wasn't read.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The forecast:** Horne warned *"that the UK will likely face AI cyberattacks on critical infrastructure by 2028, potentially including ransomware."* Digg notes *"It is a forecast, not a report of an attack that has happened."*
- **Scale, not novelty:** *"The NCSC expects AI to scale existing hostile tactics more often than discover new vulnerabilities."*
- **Defences named:** *"updated devices, restricted administrator access and backups."*
- **Examples given:** electricity switching, water-facility sluice gates and refrigeration controls.
