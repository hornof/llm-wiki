## Summary

Anthropic's post (dated 2026-11-12) introducing Claude Meetings, in beta on Team and Enterprise plans. Claude joins a Google Meet call as a visible participant, answers questions, drafts docs and whiteboards while people talk, and leaves notes and action items on a meeting page in claude.ai. **Dates:** Anthropic's 11-10 [[claude-sheets-whiteboards-data-2026-11-10|Sheets and Whiteboards article]] listed Meetings as "coming soon". TechCrunch (https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/, Aisha Malik) reported it the same day as launching in beta. Anthropic's own post and the start of the rollout are dated 11-12. Axios's 11-10 piece returned 403 and wasn't read.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **What it does:** *"Claude can now join your video calls as a visible participant, follow the conversation and start on work during the meeting."* By default *"Claude answers in writing"*, and it can use connected tools with permission.
- **Availability:** *"starting to roll out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans."* Google Meet only for now; other platforms are *"coming soon."* On Team plans it's on by default and an admin can turn it off. On Enterprise an admin turns it on.
- **Excluded during beta:** organizations with *"zero data retention (ZDR), HIPAA-ready configuration, or customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK)."*
- **Outside guests:** Claude stays out of calls with external invitees and will *"leave if someone from outside joins partway through"* unless an admin allows it. On those calls it posts its notice but doesn't reply in chat, and *"what guests say is included in the notes and transcript."*
- **What it keeps:** *"It saves a transcript, summary, and screen descriptions, not audio."* Anthropic tells users to *"make sure everyone on your calls is OK with being transcribed."*
- **Permissions:** for connected tools *"It asks the first time, and a yes applies going forward."* For spoken requests, *"Claude asks each time to confirm who’s speaking."*
- **Pricing in beta:** notes, transcripts and answers are free on Team and Enterprise, except answers that use connected tools. Artifacts made in a call are *"free through February 10, 2027."*
- **Partners for transcripts:** Gong, Attention, Granola, Fireflies.ai, Otter and Plaud, *"so you’ll soon be able to send a meeting’s transcript to the tools your team already uses."* Not yet live.
- **TechCrunch's framing:** the launch comes *"just a few weeks after OpenAI unveiled a series of workplace tools"*, with *"both companies compete to turn their products into office suites of sorts to take on Microsoft and Google."*
- **Why it matters here:** meeting notetakers are the partners named above, and Claude now does their core job inside the plan. That's the [[claude-sheets-whiteboards-data-2026-11-10|11-10 pattern]] again, an office-suite feature competing with tools that also plug into Claude. [Judgment.]
