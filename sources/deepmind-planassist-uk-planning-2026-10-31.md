## Summary

Google DeepMind, 2026-10-31, by Owen Larter and David Thacker. PlanAssist, built on Gemini with the UK government, Google Cloud and Faculty, drafts assessments of householder planning applications (extensions, loft conversions) for English councils. Officers keep the decision. After a pilot in Barnet, Camden and Dorset since June, it moves into a 12-month beta on live applications. All figures are DeepMind's own, from the pilot.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Time saved:** it *"can cut the time planning officers spend assessing householder applications by up to 58%"*.
- **Expansion:** it *"is to be made available to an additional 12 local planning authorities across England."*
- **Why this work:** *"Routine householder applications account for up to 70% of planning officers’ workloads."*
- **What it does:** validates documents and drawings, checks heritage constraints and local and national policy, checks earlier objections, then drafts *"fully cited reports that can be edited by planning officers"*.
- **Acceptance:** *"more than 70% of the reports generated by the tool"* were *"approved by officers with no edits required."*
- **Reading it:** the human-in-the-loop design and the edit-free acceptance rate are the useful numbers. The 58% is a ceiling ("up to"), not an average, and no error rate on the approved reports is given.
