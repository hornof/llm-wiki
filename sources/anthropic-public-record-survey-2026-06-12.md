## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **What it is:** *"a new survey series, Anthropic Public Record, to understand how the public thinks and feels about AI"*. The first wave was *"fielded in November and December of 2025 with nearly 52,000 Americans."* It will be repeated, and Anthropic plans to expand outside the US.
- **Method:** *"a nationally representative online survey in November and December of 2025 of 51,993 Americans, sourced from YouGov and weighted to US Census benchmarks."* National margin of error *"±0.6 percentage points"*.
- **Hopes:** *"Nearly half (48%) of Americans ranked curing diseases like cancer or Alzheimer’s as one of their top three hopes for AI"*.
- **Fears:** *"AI-induced job loss was the most common fear in every state, held by 64% of Americans."* Next were cognitive dependency (*"56%"*) and misinformation (*"52%"*).
- **Use lowers worry:** people who use AI at work every day are less worried about job loss than people who don't use it at all, *"54% versus 70%."* Worry about job loss rises with education.
- **Trust and regulation:** *"Only 15% of Americans said they trust AI companies to make decisions about how AI is developed and used"*, the lowest of any institution tested. *"71% of Americans say the government should be involved in the development and regulation of AI"*, a majority in both parties.
- **Heavy users:** *"about 6% of Americans used AI every day for both work and personal life."* They're less worried across the board but want government involvement at about the national rate.
- **Limits:** *"Anthropic Public Record captures what Americans believed about AI in late 2025. We are treating it as a baseline."*
- **For the owner:** the 54% versus 70% job-worry gap between daily users and non-users is a useful number for any talk on AI adoption. It's correlation; Anthropic offers several explanations and doesn't pick one. [Judgment.]
