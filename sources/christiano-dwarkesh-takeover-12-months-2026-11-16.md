## Summary

Dwarkesh Patel interviews Paul Christiano (2026-11-16). Christiano heads the Alignment Research Center, was head of AI safety at the US AI Safety Institute, led alignment at OpenAI, and recently joined OpenAI's board; he speaks as a private citizen. He puts the risk of AI takeover within a year nearer 10% than 1% and argues labs should pause algorithmic progress until they can keep their weights and methods from being stolen. Read from the episode page and transcript; opinion on timelines, not evidence.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Risk over the next year** (0:00:00): *"I would say we are looking more like a 10% risk than a 1% risk over the next year."* If developers go full speed without precautions, *"you’re looking at significantly more than a 10% risk."*
- **Timeline:** *"maybe a 1/3 chance that we have superintelligence within one year on the current development trajectory, if we do not slow."* The mechanism he worries about is AI development itself becoming automated.
- **Pause, for security reasons:** asked whether labs should pause, *"I think we should."* He means algorithmic progress specifically: *"it would be better right now, in the current environment, for AI developers to pause further algorithmic progress on those problems until their security posture is adequate"*. Otherwise an adversary could steal a model mid-takeoff, and labs couldn't slow down even if they wanted to.
- **On alignment** (episode description): fixing reward hacks and better graders help for the next few model generations, but *"no single current approach has better than even odds of letting us control superintelligence."*
- **Other segments:** how a takeover could happen (0:20:49), the sim-to-real gap (1:11:32), and *"METR says AI progress doubled, so why is revenue growth slowing?"* (1:22:23).
