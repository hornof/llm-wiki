## Summary

[[andrew-ng|Andrew Ng]] (DeepLearning.AI) publishes **"The AI Engineering Skills Map"** (2026-08-14) — a synthesis of the **four most important AI-engineering skills**, derived from *"an analysis of over 10,000 job postings, dozens of structured interviews with AI experts / hiring managers / recruiters, surveys, and other online data."* Anchor for [[ai-engineering-skills]]. Framed as **skills, not the "AI Engineer" role** — all developers need them, the way all developers need cloud skills. *(Primary fetched.)*

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **The four skills**: (1) **Building & deploying AI applications**, (2) **Software-engineering fundamentals**, (3) **Using coding agents**, (4) **Shaping the build**.
- **What makes AI apps different**: *"unpredictable outputs"* — the skill is statistical measure/steer/govern to make behavior predictable; **disciplined evals + error-analysis loops** are the core sub-skill.
- **Fundamentals are steering leverage**: *"an inexperienced developer who vibe-codes without knowing the tradeoffs their coding agent is making will often get poor ones, because they don't know what context to give"* — fundamentals let you steer *"in the precise language of software engineering."*
- **Using coding agents** = mental model of limits + intervene-vs-leave-alone, context management, plan-vs-execute tradeoff, **verifiers/evals to close loops**, spec discipline (*and when not to bother*), **multi-agent orchestration**, avoiding *"an agent messing up your production database"*, and *routines to keep trying new tools.*
- **Shaping the build**: *"given a clear spec, coding agents are rapidly improving at delivering to it. Thus, our work is shifting toward deciding what should be in the spec"* — product sense + business/customer context + **ownership/agency** (MVP-fast vs build-careful judgment). *"Engineers should no longer expect a pixel-perfect design and asked only to implement it."*
- **Meta-skill**: *"a mindset of continuous learning"* — best practices churn.
