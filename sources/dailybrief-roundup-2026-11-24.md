## Folded here

- **Anthropic slips below its $83 IPO price** (Digg citing Bloomberg, CNBC, Reuters). On its fourth trading day the stock hit *"an early-session low of $79.42"*, about 29% below its first trade. Canaccord ($135), Oppenheimer ($150) and Wedbush ($120) started coverage bullish. Dario and Daniela Amodei *"each sold more than 3.37 million shares, worth over $280 million apiece"*, to the underwriters at the offer price, as set out in the prospectus. MSCI adds Anthropic to its indexes *"after the November 30 close"*. Added to [[anthropic]].
- **Huang apologizes; his direct reports will review him** (Digg citing WIRED and the WSJ). At his first all-hands since returning, Huang acknowledged a code-of-conduct violation and *“very serious personal mistakes.”* He said he was in therapy and that *"his direct reports would send reviews of his behavior to the board."* He also thanked the Trump administration for quick access to intelligence that helped the board. Added to [[nvidia]] and [[jensen-huang]].
- **Trump's Nvidia stake** (Digg citing the FT). The rally after Huang's return could have added *"as much as $15 million on paper"* to Trump's holdings, assuming the largest stake his filings allow (*"as many as 370,000 Nvidia shares"*); the low estimate is 32,000 shares. Trump: *“I made the deal.”* Added to [[nvidia]].
- **Some OpenAI and Anthropic staff doubt unreleased models are safe to ship** (Digg citing Axios, *"more than two dozen sources"*). Debates include keeping frontier models internal or stopping training; *"These are reported disagreements, not announced company decisions"*. No evaluations published. Added to [[frontier-ai-governance]].
- **Tidal removes fully AI-generated music from December 9** (Digg). About *"27 million tracks"* carry the tag, and *"96% received no streams in the past three months"*. AI-assisted tracks stay. No page change.
- **Meta's Muse launches in the UK** (Digg). Meta's agent for shopping, bookings and admin is on mobile, web and WhatsApp; it asks before sending email or buying, and users must switch off training on their interactions themselves. No page change.
- **Ex-OpenAI engineer's recordings** (Digg citing Engadget and the FT). Prosecutors say Abdulrahman Alfozan recorded *"more than 160 hours"* of material, including OpenAI meetings, over three weeks in September. He has pleaded not guilty and denies acting for Saudi Arabia. Added to [[openai]].
- **Inkling 2 Mini benchmarks** (Digg citing @victormustar). *"48.2% on DeepSWE, ranking first below 32B parameters"*, and *"79.2% on SWE-bench Verified, ranking first below 128B"*. Self-reported by a Hugging Face employee, not Thinking Machines. Added to [[inkling]].
- **domdomegg/computer-use-mcp** (GitHub). An MCP server that gives Claude mouse and keyboard control of a local computer; the README says to treat it *"like giving a hyperactive toddler access to your computer"* and recommends 720p. Compare [[greptile-trex-computer-use-2026-11-23]]. No page change.
- **mlc-ai/tx-verify** (GitHub). Proves compiled PTX GPU kernels safe and race-free in Lean, listing any assumption beyond a shared core in the kernel's theorem for a person to review. Same pattern as the quantum work above. No page change.
