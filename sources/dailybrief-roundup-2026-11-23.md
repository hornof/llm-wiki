## Folded here

- **Oregon and Virginia sue over Grok safeguards** (Digg citing AP, WIRED, CNBC). Separate consumer-protection suits against SpaceX and X Corp. allege Grok let users make nonconsensual sexual images, including child sexual abuse material, despite promised safeguards. Virginia alleges the companies marketed a *“spicy mode”* and that its investigators found the image safeguards *“inconsistent and ineffective”*. Both states seek injunctions and penalties; Virginia asks *"$2,500 for each willful violation"*. Musk's lawyers have argued elsewhere that Grok is *“a neutral tool, subject to user control”*. Added to [[xai]].
- **Sierra raises $1.75B at $30B** (Digg; Sierra on X). Series F led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, up from *"$15.8B in May"* (Bloomberg via Techmeme). Sierra says *"we now work with almost 50% of the Fortune 50"* and that ARR went from $200M to $400M in five months (*"only 5 months to double from $200M to $400M"*). It charges per outcome, not per token. Digg notes the announcement doesn't say the round has closed. No entity page.
- **Agon raises $70M** (Digg citing Bloomberg). The London defense-simulation startup is valued at *"$450 million"* and builds models to test how drones and military robots behave. No entity page.
- **AI Mode connects Wallet, Drive and Gemini** (Digg citing 9to5Google). Wallet is rolling out now and is *"read-only"*; Drive and Gemini chats are coming. No page change.
- **Gemini 4 Neon recreates Turing's study in 3D** (Digg). DeepMind's demo in Antigravity includes *"a running Universal Turing Machine"*; Digg notes no word on how it was checked. Demo only; no page change.
- **ImpasseBench** (GitHub). Tests whether coding agents stop and escalate when the environment makes a task impossible (read-only files, full disk, lost sandbox): *"102 SWE-smith repair tasks"*, *"12 injected environment faults"*, *"19,371 trajectories from 8 models"*, scored by rules with no LLM judge. No results in the README excerpt saved.
- **V3R1T4S** (GitHub, elder-plinius). An agent harness that runs overnight against open problems. The README claims faster matrix-multiplication schemes, two cipher challenges solved and Lehmer's totient bound pushed to *"a computer-verified 10⁵⁰"*, *"53 results in all"*, mostly not yet accepted by the table owners. Self-reported; not checked. Compare [[ai-for-science]].
