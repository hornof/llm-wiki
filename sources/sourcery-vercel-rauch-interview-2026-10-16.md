## Summary

Sourcery (Molly O'Shea, 2026-10-16) interview with [[guillermo-rauch|Guillermo Rauch]] at Vercel's San Francisco office. The page summarises it. Two figures matter for the wiki: 60% of Vercel deployments are now agentic, up from 3% at the start of 2026, and roughly 80% of tokens through **Vercel AI Gateway** go to open models, up from mostly proprietary at the start of the year.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Open-model share:** "roughly 80%" of AI Gateway token volume (Rauch). This answers the question the [[dailybrief-roundup-2026-10-16|10-16 roundup]] raised: it is **gateway traffic**, not all inference Vercel's customers run. A single-day record of 78.4% appeared in Rauch's September post on X. Vercel publishes anonymised gateway usage under a Creative Commons licence.
- **Agentic deployments:** 60%, up from 3%. How a deployment is classed as agentic isn't explained; the article says "coding agents" in one place and "agentic" in another.
- **Company figures (secondary):** $9.3B valuation at the last round; $600M annualised revenue, up 148% (The Information); more than 485,000 paying teams; about 800 employees.
- **Deployments:** 1B in about ten years, then 1.5B more in the last ten months.
- **Sandbox bounty:** $1M challenge, 2026-08-18 to 09-01. It drew 1,285 reports and paid out $242,000. The article says two Linux networking-stack vulnerabilities were found; Vercel's own post says "one Linux kernel zero day."
- **Caveats:** the 60% and 80% figures come with no measurement period or method. Revenue and valuation are reported second-hand.
