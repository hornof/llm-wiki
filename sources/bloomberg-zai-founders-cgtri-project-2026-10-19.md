## Summary

Digg summary of a Bloomberg report (2026-10-19). Four [[z-ai|Z.ai]] founders, Li Juanzi, Tang Jie, Xu Bin and CEO Zhang Peng, took part in a 2018–2021 research project co-funded by the China General Technology Research Institute (CGTRI) and the National Natural Science Foundation. Britain's MI5 later alleged that CGTRI has "very strong ties" to Chinese intelligence. The project built tools to analyse social-media posts, identify events and map people's connections, including a knowledge base of "China-related key persons."

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Project:** January 2018 to December 2021. Budget 2.94M yuan (about $437,000 at the time). Led by Li Juanzi.
- **Also involved:** a CGTRI engineer, Cao Weibing, and an unnamed Southeast University professor.
- **Caveats in the report itself:** Bloomberg says it is unclear what the founders knew about CGTRI's alleged ties. The MI5 alert did not mention Z.ai. China's UK embassy called MI5's allegations "baseless."
- **Z.ai and the founders** did not respond to Bloomberg.
