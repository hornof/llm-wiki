## Summary

In a 2026-10-31 X post, summarized by Digg, Elon Musk described two planned SpaceX/xAI compute clusters: Gun Range for training and Spam for inference, about 420,000 GB300s between them. The figures are plans and the dates are targets.

## Key Claims / Takeaways

- **Sizes:** *"220,000 GB300s in Gun Range for training and about 200,000 in Spam for inference"*.
- **Dates:** Gun Range operational by *"the end of November 2026"*, Spam in December.
- **Comparison:** Gun Range would match Macroharder and Minihard, each described as 220,000 GB300s for training.
- **Caveat (Digg):** *"the dates are operational targets, not confirmation that either cluster is operating."*
